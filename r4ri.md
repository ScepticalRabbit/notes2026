## Module 1 — Contributions to the generation of new ideas, tools, methodologies or knowledge

LF leads research on simulation-driven experimental design, validation and uncertainty quantification for fusion components through a £3.2M UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship, *A Computer Aided Validation Framework for Digital Design Qualification* ([UKRI MR/Y015916/1](https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=MR%2FY015916%2F1)). His work develops methods for designing data-rich experiments specifically to challenge and validate engineering simulations, providing directly relevant expertise for validation of the tightly coupled thermo-electro-magnetic-mechanical models developed in this project. This includes the open-source [Pyvale](https://github.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/pyvale) virtual engineering laboratory for simulation-driven experimental design and uncertainty analysis, and [Riley](https://github.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/riley-raster), a high-performance framework for generating realistic synthetic measurements from higher-order finite-element solutions. Pyvale's DIC implementation has demonstrated scalable analysis of gigapixel experimental images ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12941)), while LF has also contributed to development and demonstration of probabilistic validation methodologies for tightly coupled fusion-component simulations ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11886)).

## Module 2 — Contributions to the development of others and maintenance of effective working relationships

LF has built and technically leads a multidisciplinary experimental–computational research capability at UKAEA South Yorkshire, integrating experimental mechanics, simulation, scientific computing, statistics and machine learning. He currently line-manages researchers from early-career engineers to lead engineers and provides technical leadership across the wider Future Leaders Fellowship team. He has mentored staff through successful promotion, professional registration and development of external fellowship and grant applications, and supervises doctoral projects across Imperial College London, Oxford, Liverpool and Swansea. These collaborations deliberately bring complementary expertise into UKAEA, including statistics and machine learning for experimental optimisation, multiphysics simulation validation and digital-twin VVUQ. This experience is directly relevant to the proposed project, where close integration between the academic multiphysics-development teams and UKAEA's experimental-validation activity will be essential.

## Module 3 — Contributions to the wider research and innovation community

LF contributes to the wider experimental-mechanics and engineering-validation community as a member of the British Society for Strain Measurement national committee, through work with the International Digital Image Correlation Society on the Stereo DIC Challenge, and as a UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship peer reviewer. Within UKAEA he has contributed to Fusion Technology Division strategy and to development of cross-divisional virtual-qualification programmes, helping establish simulation-driven experimental design and validation as strategic capabilities. He also serves on the University of Sheffield Materials Engineering Industrial Advisory Board, linking fusion-industry needs with university engineering education and research.

## Module 4 — Contributions to broader research or innovation users and audiences and towards wider societal benefit

LF's research emphasises translation of validation methods into reusable engineering tools, open research infrastructure and benchmark data. He founded and leads development of the MIT-licensed [Pyvale](https://github.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/pyvale) platform, designed to make simulation-driven experimental design, measurement simulation and validation workflows accessible to engineers and researchers beyond UKAEA. Its DIC capability is already being used externally for large-scale scanning-electron-microscopy datasets and has demonstrated gigapixel-scale analysis ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12941)). He has also contributed to an open probabilistic-validation benchmark for fusion components in which experimental, simulation and validation datasets were released specifically for reuse in development of new model-validation methodologies ([preprint and dataset description](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11886)). These activities support wider uptake, reproducibility and transfer of advanced validation methods into both academic and engineering practice.

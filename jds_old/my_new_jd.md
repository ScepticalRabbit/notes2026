# Job Description: Strategic Research Lead – Engineering Validation

* 
**Career Family:** Engineering / Scientific 


* **Role Title:** Strategic Research Lead (Engineering Validation & Digital Qualification)
* **Grade:** Grade J (Proposed)
* 
**Reports To:** Head of Division or Department Manager 


* **Number of Direct Reports:** 4 FTE (Matrix HR management)
* **Total Direct and Indirect Reports:** 8 FTE (Direct Technical Authority)

## Overall Purpose:

* **Why does the role exist?** To lead the strategic development and organizational establishment of a core **Engineering Validation** capability that bridges the gap between high-fidelity simulations and experimental physics.
* **What does the role aim to achieve?** It aims to create and authorize the methodologies required for digital design qualification, reducing the time and cost of complex experimental campaigns for next-generation engineering systems.
* **What wouldn’t happen if the role didn’t exist?** The organization would lack a unified, multi-year strategic framework for **Verification, Validation, and Uncertainty Quantification (VVUQ)**, leading to fragmented research efforts and slower commercial delivery timelines.
* **What does the role contribute?** It delivers a world-leading competitive advantage by establishing proprietary, high-performance computational-experimental frameworks that support both divisional goals and long-term organizational viability.

## Accountabilities:

* **Strategic Capability Leadership:** Define, authorize, and lead a multi-year (5–7 year) strategic roadmap for engineering validation that aligns with divisional objectives and commercial milestones.
* **Programmatic Financial Oversight:** Independently secure, authorize, and manage a multi-million pound (£3M+) research and innovation portfolio, ensuring long-term financial stability and resource growth for the capability.
* **Technical Authority & Autonomy:** Act as the final technical authority for an interdisciplinary "strike team" (8+ FTE) within a matrix environment, with the autonomy to determine delivery methods and second resources to high-priority cross-divisional tasks.
* **Methodological Innovation:** Direct the development of bespoke, high-performance computational architectures and data science interfaces to solve problems of the highest complexity where no commercial precedent exists.
* **Influence & Reputation:** Represent the organization as an authoritative expert on national and international technical committees (e.g., BSSM) and serve as an expert peer reviewer for major national funding bodies to shape industry standards.
* **Mentorship & Pipeline Development:** Lead a technical mentorship framework that builds organizational capability at all levels, specifically coaching senior staff to secure their own independent funding and developing the graduate pipeline.
* **Strategic Impact & ROI:** Identify and exploit opportunities for cross-sector impact, translating core research into broader industrial and economic benefits to maximize return on investment.

## Specific Qualifications/Experience:

* **Academic & Professional Standing:** PhD in Mechanical Engineering (or a relevant STEM field) and Chartered Engineer (CEng) status with a relevant professional body (e.g., IMechE).
* **Expertise Mastery:** Authoritative subject matter expertise across a unique combination of experimental solid mechanics, digital imaging diagnostics, and performant systems-level software engineering.
* **Track Record:** An established international reputation for research excellence, evidenced by a significant volume of peer-reviewed articles (20+) and a proven history of winning and leading major research grants.
* **Strategic Thinking:** Demonstrable experience in "starting from first principles" to conceptualize and deliver new technical frameworks that integrate diverse multi-physics disciplines.
* **Soft Skills & Leadership:** Advanced ability to lead multi-disciplinary teams through high-ambiguity challenges and influence senior stakeholders at the Board or Governmental level.

## Additional Duties:

* **National & International Representation:** Serve on national advisory boards and committees to represent the organization’s interests and shape the future of the field.
* **Academic Pipeline:** Maintain a visiting lectureship and act as a primary or industry co-supervisor for a portfolio of PhD students to ensure knowledge transfer to the next generation.
* **Review Panels:** Act as a specialist on independent review panels and steering committees for high-value national grants and peer-reviewed journals.
* **Mission Translation:** Responsible for the translation of research findings into practical engineering standards that can be adopted by external industrial partners and commercial start-ups.

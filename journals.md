## Candidate computational journals

- **Engineering with Computers**
  - Publisher: Springer
  - 2024 Impact Factor: **4.9**
  - CiteScore: **16.5**
  - Median first decision: **7 days**
  - Best when the paper emphasizes:
    - simulation-based engineering
    - algorithms and workflows
    - engineering software
    - computational methodology
  - My take: **best overall fit** for your first rasteriser paper. 

- **Advances in Engineering Software**
  - Publisher: Elsevier
  - 2024 Impact Factor: **5.7**
  - CiteScore: **9.2**
  - First decision: **3 days**
  - First decision after review: **48 days**
  - Acceptance rate: **6%**
  - Best when the paper emphasizes:
    - engineering software
    - implementation and performance
    - scalable computational workflows
  - My take: **strongest backup** if you want a more software-heavy framing.

## My recommendation by paper

- **FE rasteriser / computational methods paper**
  - Best target: **Engineering with Computers**
  - Strong alternatives:
    - **Advances in Engineering Software**
    - **Finite Elements in Analysis and Design** 

- **DIC UQ / interpolation bias / supersampling paper**
  - Best targets:
    - **Experimental Mechanics**
    - **Strain**
  - Strong alternatives:
    - **Journal of Strain Analysis for Engineering Design**
    - **Optics and Lasers in Engineering** 

- **Procedural speckle generation / synthetic pattern paper**
  - Best targets:
    - **Engineering with Computers** if it is algorithm/software first
    - **Optics and Lasers in Engineering** if it is image-synthesis/optical-method first
  - Strong alternatives:
    - **Experimental Mechanics**
    - **Strain**

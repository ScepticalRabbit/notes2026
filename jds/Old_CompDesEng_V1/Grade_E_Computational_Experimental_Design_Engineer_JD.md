# Computational Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Computational Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** E  
**Reports to:** Senior Computational Experimental Design Engineer (or higher)  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

Develop and apply computational methods and reusable engineering tools for the design, simulation and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. The role combines computational experimental design; sensor and measurement modelling; verification, validation and uncertainty quantification (VVUQ); and performance-aware programming to help engineers obtain the strongest possible evidence from finite experimental resources.

By improving the information gained from experiments, the role supports risk-aware engineering decisions while reducing unnecessary testing, cost, material use and demand on specialist facilities. This contributes to sustainable and commercially viable fusion energy and creates UK economic benefit through the transfer of computational methods and design tools into fusion engineering and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

A Computational Experimental Design Engineer implements, verifies and improves clearly defined computational methods and components under the guidance of senior colleagues.

## Accountabilities

- Develop clearly defined computational features and performant reusable components for the design, simulation and analysis of validation experiments, working from technical specifications and within established scientific and computational architectures.
- Implement established mathematical and numerical methods for computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, and VVUQ.
- Translate mathematical descriptions, algorithms and engineering requirements into performant, tested, clear and maintainable computational implementations using Python and, where required, suitable compiled languages.
- Develop and maintain accessible Python interfaces to performant computational components for use by fusion engineers, experimentalists and researchers.
- Profile computational modules, algorithms and workflows to identify performance limitations, implement appropriate local improvements, and evaluate alternatives using reproducible performance benchmarks under senior guidance.
- Develop and apply unit tests, numerical verification cases, regression tests and reference comparisons to demonstrate correctness, robustness and performance.
- Implement and apply sensor models, measurement uncertainty models, validation metrics and uncertainty propagation methods selected or approved by senior colleagues, documenting assumptions, limitations and sources of uncertainty.
- Analyse simulation and experimental data to assess measurement uncertainty, information gain, parameter identifiability and validation evidence, investigating unexpected behaviour and proposing improvements within the assigned component or work package.
- Work with experimentalists, simulation engineers, measurement specialists and researchers to understand requirements and implement defined computational solutions.
- Take responsibility for the quality, correctness, documentation and timely delivery of discrete outputs within larger work packages.
- Make implementation decisions within defined parameters and escalate decisions concerning methodology, architecture, programme risk or significant resources.
- Contribute technical content to publications, conference outputs, reports, and support transfer of methods into fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience gained within a structured research environment, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Demonstrable ability to translate clearly defined mathematical, scientific or engineering requirements into working computational implementations.
- Intermediate knowledge of Python for scientific or engineering computation, including numerical array programming, testing, documentation and reusable modules or packages.
- Basic knowledge of C and its use in interfaces that enable interoperability between programming languages.
- Basic knowledge of at least one compiled systems programming language with explicit control over memory and data representation, such as C, C++, C3, Odin or Zig.
- Foundational knowledge of performance-aware programming, including numerical array operations, memory allocation, memory layout, data movement, profiling and benchmarking.
- Knowledge of software testing and numerical verification practices.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Knowledge of relevant numerical and mathematical principles, which may include numerical methods, linear algebra, probability, statistics, optimisation, inference or uncertainty propagation.
- Ability to implement and apply established mathematical or engineering methods selected by a senior colleague.
- Ability to recognise, document and communicate assumptions, limitations and sources of uncertainty.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- Demonstrable knowledge or practical experience in at least one of the following, with the ability and motivation to develop across the others: computational experimental design; sensor, imaging or measurement-system modelling; measurement uncertainty and uncertainty propagation; VVUQ; numerical optimisation, statistical inference or information-theoretic methods; image-based measurement, computational imaging or synthetic data generation; or performance-aware numerical or scientific computing.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- Experience contributing to research, engineering development or technical investigation within a structured environment.
- Ability to analyse technical results, maintain reproducible records and draw evidence-based conclusions.
- Ability to communicate clearly across engineering and scientific disciplines and contribute to technical reports, publications and conference outputs.
- Ability to plan and prioritise assigned activities, work independently within defined parameters and seek guidance appropriately.

## Additional Duties

- Present technical work internally and, where appropriate, at external meetings, workshops or conferences.
- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- Complete role-specific training required to work safely and effectively.
- No line-management responsibility and no delegated budgetary authority.
- Provide informal technical support to colleagues, students or new team members.

# Lead Computational Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Lead Computational Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** H  
**Reports to:** Principal Computational Experimental Design Engineer (or higher)  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** Likely  

## Overall Purpose

Lead coordinated computational capabilities and substantial programmes of work for the design, simulation and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. Define integrated technical approaches and architectures across multiple projects spanning computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware computational engineering.

The role is accountable for coordinated technical delivery, capability development and effective deployment of multidisciplinary teams and resources. It enables stronger validation evidence, more efficient experimental programmes and the development of sustainable and commercially viable fusion technologies with wider scientific and UK economic impact.

A Lead Computational Experimental Design Engineer leads coordinated computational capabilities across major projects, defines integrated technical approaches and architectures, and is accountable for the delivery and development of multidisciplinary teams and substantial programmes of work.

## Accountabilities

- Define and lead substantial programmes of technical work across several projects or related capability areas, setting objectives, priorities, delivery structures and assurance requirements.
- Establish integrated methodological and computational approaches across experimental design, measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware computing.
- Define and coordinate architectures across related libraries, toolboxes and engineering workflows, resolving cross-project dependencies and technical conflicts.
- Direct development of new methods and computational capabilities for complex problems with limited precedent.
- Establish technical assurance, performance and validation strategies across major projects, and approve significant technical decisions within delegated areas.
- Lead multidisciplinary teams and work-package leaders, coordinate internal and external contributors, and ensure coherent delivery against organisational objectives.
- Plan and manage substantial budgets, people, computing resources and experimental activity across major projects.
- Identify and manage significant technical and programme risks, balancing scientific ambition, engineering need, resource constraints and delivery priorities.
- Lead major research proposals, coordinated publication portfolios and strategic external collaborations.
- Build partnerships with universities, industry and research organisations to extend capability, attract investment and accelerate adoption.
- Shape divisional technical practice, investment priorities and capability plans.
- Line-manage or matrix-manage engineers and researchers, develop Senior-level staff and establish capability and succession plans.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant quantitative discipline.
- Extensive relevant experience or a doctorate supported by a strong record of technical, research and programme leadership.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Deep knowledge of scientific computing, compiled-language interoperability and performance-aware computational architecture.
- Proven ability to define integrated architectures and technical standards across multiple libraries, projects or teams.
- Experience assuring performance, correctness, interoperability and long-term maintainability of substantial computational capabilities.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Expert knowledge in at least one core capability area and advanced breadth across the others.
- Proven ability to establish integrated technical approaches for complex multidisciplinary programmes.
- Experience approving validation, uncertainty and experimental-design strategies for significant projects.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- Recognised subject-matter expertise with the ability to coordinate several specialist areas into a coherent capability.
- Experience leading development of new methods and resolving complex issues with limited precedent.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- A strong record of leading major research proposals, coordinated publications and national or international collaborations.
- Experience leading substantial multidisciplinary programmes and managing budgets, resources and technical risks.
- Ability to influence senior stakeholders, negotiate priorities and represent the organisation externally.
- Demonstrable experience developing teams, mentoring senior engineers and creating sustainable technical capability.

## Additional Duties

- Likely to hold line-management or matrix-management responsibility for a multidisciplinary team.
- May hold delegated authority for substantial budgets, resources and technical approvals.
- Represent the organisation in senior national and international technical forums and collaborations.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

# Job descriptions: Dos and Donts

## Purpose of the Role
### Do
Think about:

Why does the role exist?

What does the role aim to achieve?

What wouldn’t happen if the role didn’t exist?

What does the role contribute to or deliver for UKAEA? (consider UKAEA mission and goals, and Divisional goals and high-level objectives).

This section should not be more than one paragraph with two or three sentences explaining the purpose of the role.

### Don't
Tell us what UKAEA does.

Provide an overview or an explanation of what the Division/Unit/Group/Team does.

Provide a list of accountabilities.

Go into depth on role responsibilities.

Provide details of the skills or attributes needed for the role.

## Specific Qualifications & Experience
### Do 
Capture the minimum required relevant qualification needed to deliver the outputs of the role.

Refer to the extent of experience, job knowledge, technical skills, specialist knowledge e.g. demonstratable, track record of, extensive, familiar with, proficient, advanced, expert, etc.

Include the soft skills required to successfully perform in the role e.g. leadership, communications, adaptability, build rapport, planning, prioritising, problem-solving, creative thinking, etc.

### Don't
Specify the number of years' experience (use demonstrable, proficient, extensive, knowledge of, etc.)

Base qualifications/experience on a current incumbent.

## Budge
### Do 
Consider what the role does in terms of setting, approving, and managing budgets – or state Not applicable.

Advise of approximate budget up to £xxx and state if it is an annual divisional budget or project/ programme budget.

State the input on spending decision – appointee will have e.g. sole or joint or indirect decision making.

If budgetary spend is multi-year state number of years.

### Don't
Get hung up about being exact on budgetary figure (e.g. approx. up to circa, annual spend is sufficient)

## Additional Duties
### Do 
Include specific appointments e.g. Responsible Officer, Authority to Operate Holder,

Include if the role needs to be on-call/do shift work, wear PPE, deputise for, support on.

Consider if the role needs serve on advisory boards/committees or to represent at forums, conferences.

Consider if the role has to undertake national or international travel.

Include if qualifications need to be undertaken and completed specifically required for the role.

### Don't
Include one off tasks.

Include everything you can think of ‘just in case’.

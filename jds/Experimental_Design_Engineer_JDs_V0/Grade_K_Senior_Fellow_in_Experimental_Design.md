# Senior Fellow in Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Senior Fellow in Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** K  
**Reports to:** Director or higher  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Senior Fellow in Experimental Design provides internationally recognised technical leadership, establishes new research directions and shapes organisation-level strategy and external influence in the discipline.

The purpose of this role is to provide internationally recognised scientific and engineering leadership in experimental design, establishing new fields and shaping the organisation's long-term technical direction, external standing and impact. The role operates as an organisation-level authority across integrated computational and experimental validation. It creates new methods, research directions and strategic partnerships spanning experimental design, advanced diagnostics, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, performance-aware computing and evidence-led engineering. It influences major organisational priorities and provides authoritative challenge where programmes face novel, uncertain or strategically important technical decisions.

By defining the state of the art and translating it into sustainable organisational and industrial capability, the role advances fusion engineering, strengthens the UK's scientific leadership and accelerates innovation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors. 

## Accountabilities

- Establish and lead internationally significant research directions in integrated computational and experimental design.
- Act as an organisation-level authority for major scientific, engineering and validation challenges with little or no precedent.
- Shape long-term organisational strategy, policy, investment and external positioning in experimental design and validation.
- Sponsor or direct major programmes, capabilities and technical leaders across multiple divisions, institutions or sectors.
- Create new methods, diagnostics, software architectures, validation frameworks or experimental paradigms with substantial impact.
- Provide authoritative scrutiny and challenge to major organisational programmes, evidence strategies and engineering decisions.
- Influence allocation and prioritisation of substantial organisational resources and strategic investments.
- Originate and lead major national and international partnerships, funding initiatives, advisory activities and research networks.
- Represent the organisation at the highest technical levels with government, industry, academia, funders and international bodies.
- Translate frontier research into adoption across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.
- Develop senior technical leaders and future authorities across the organisation and wider community.
- Protect and enhance the organisation's long-term scientific reputation, capability and strategic influence.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of sustained original achievement would normally be expected.
- Chartered status, Fellowship of an appropriate professional or learned body, or equivalent international standing, would normally be expected where relevant.
- Extensive, broad and comprehensive experience gained through an internationally distinguished scientific, engineering or research career.

### Computational Knowledge and Experience

- Internationally recognised authority in advanced computational methods, scientific software, numerical architecture or performance-aware computing relevant to engineering validation.
- Proven record of creating or directing computational methods and software capabilities that have materially influenced research, engineering practice or industry.
- Ability to judge and shape long-term computational strategy across software, hardware, assurance, interoperability and sustainability.
- Broad understanding of emerging central processing unit (CPU), accelerator, distributed and data-intensive computing and their strategic relevance.
- Experience establishing standards or principles adopted beyond a single team, programme or organisation.

### Experimental Knowledge and Experience

- Internationally recognised authority in experimental engineering, measurement science, advanced diagnostics, validation or an integrated combination of these.
- Sustained record of creating or directing experimental approaches that have materially changed scientific or engineering practice.
- Deep knowledge of measurement assurance, calibration, traceability, uncertainty and evidence quality at programme and organisational scale.
- Ability to shape long-term strategy for facilities, instrumentation, diagnostics and experimental capability.
- Broad authority across several experimental domains, supported by exceptional depth in at least one.

### Engineering Analysis and Validation Knowledge and Experience

- International authority in experimental design, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, measurement modelling, engineering validation or a closely related field.
- Proven ability to resolve unprecedented, multidisciplinary challenges requiring original analytical, interpretative and evaluative thinking.
- Ability to define new frameworks for connecting evidence, uncertainty, risk and strategic engineering decisions.
- Experience shaping national or international standards, policy, strategy or research agendas.
- Exceptional judgement across scientific, engineering, organisational and societal consequences.

### Research, Communication and Leadership Experience

- Internationally distinguished record of original research, major programmes, influential publications, software, datasets, facilities or engineering outcomes.
- Evidence of creating new research fields, capability areas or communities of practice.
- Experience leading through technical authority, matrix influence, major partnerships and development of other senior leaders.
- Established influence with external senior leaders in industry, academia, funders and international organisations.
- Proven record of mentoring Principal-level staff and future international leaders.

## Additional Duties

- May lead a substantial function, portfolio or network of technical teams, but line management is not a requirement.
- May hold authority over major budgets, resources, organisational approvals or strategic appointments.
- Represent the organisation in senior national and international advisory and strategic forums.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

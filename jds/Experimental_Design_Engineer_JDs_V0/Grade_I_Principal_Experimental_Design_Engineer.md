# Principal Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Principal Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** I  
**Reports to:** Head of Experimental Design or equivalent senior leader  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Principal Experimental Design Engineer leads a significant capability or project area, providing senior technical leadership and accountability for integrated computational and experimental outcomes.

The purpose of this role is to lead a significant experimental-design capability or project within a broader research and engineering portfolio, integrating computation, advanced diagnostics and practical experimentation to shape fusion engineering decisions. The role provides senior technical leadership across experimental programmes, computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, high-performance scientific computing and practical test delivery. It develops and delivers technical roadmaps, coordinates major multidisciplinary activity, may hold delegated authority for significant technical decisions and develops new engineering methdology for cases with limited precendent.

By creating stronger evidence for difficult engineering decisions, the role improves the effectiveness of major validation programmes, develops sustainable organisational capability and accelerates translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors. 

## Accountabilities

- Lead and develop a significant experimental-design capability area or a strategically important element within a wider portfolio.
- Develop and deliver technical direction, roadmaps and assurance approaches aligned with wider programme, research need and organisational objectives.
- Make and approve significant technical decisions within delegated scope, escalating decisions with wider organisational consequences.
- Coordinate integrated programmes of computational and physical experimental work spanning software, instrumentation, diagnostics, data and engineering interpretation.
- Lead development of new methods, algorithms, diagnostics or test approaches where existing capability is inadequate and there is limited precendent.
- Provide senior technical review and approval within an assigned programme or defined specialist area.
- Lead multidisciplinary collaborations and coordinate senior computational, experimental and domain specialists across projects.
- Plan and coordinate people, budgets, facilities, computing resources and experimental activity, advising on priorities and exercising authority where assigned.
- Originate and lead substantial research proposals, programmes, publications, software platforms, datasets and external partnerships.
- Contribute to divisional strategy and wider organisational capability and investment planning in experimental design and validation.
- Translate methods, software and experimental capability into strategic use across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.
- Develop technical leaders and build sustainable capability, succession and professional standards across the job family.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive and broad experience across computational research, engineering experimentation and technical leadership.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of original research and technical leadership may be advantageous.
- Chartered status or equivalent professional standing may be advantageous where relevant.

### Computational Knowledge and Experience

- Deep knowledge of scientific computing, numerical methods, systems programming and accessible interfaces to performant compiled implementations.
- Demonstrable expertise in performance-aware computational architecture across modern central processing unit (CPU), vector, parallel and accelerator platforms.
- Proven ability to define technical standards and architectures for complex, interoperating scientific libraries, methods, toolboxes and workflows.
- Experience providing authoritative assurance of correctness, robustness, performance, maintainability and operational fitness.
- Ability to sponsor and direct new computational methods while balancing research novelty, software quality, sustainability and user accessibility.

### Experimental Knowledge and Experience

- Extensive practical experience in experimental engineering, measurement science and validation.
- Proven ability to define and lead integrated experimental programmes involving complex instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, specialist facilities and multidisciplinary teams.
- Deep understanding of calibration, synchronisation, traceability, uncertainty, test readiness and data-quality governance.
- Ability to approve or challenge experimental strategies, measurement architectures and evidence packages within scope of authority.
- Advanced expertise in at least one diagnostic or experimental domain, with broad capability to lead integrated programmes across several.

### Engineering Analysis and Validation Knowledge and Experience

- Deep expertise in at least one core capability area and advanced breadth across experimental design, measurement modelling, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification and numerical simulation.
- Proven ability to select, adapt and develop methods for complex problems where objectives are broad and there is limited precendent.
- Extensive experience establishing validation and uncertainty strategies for engineering or programme decisions.
- Ability to frame experimental programmes around decision needs, evidence gaps, information value, risk and resource constraints.
- Strong judgement in balancing research novelty, technical ambition, practical feasibility, cost, schedule and evidence quality.

### Research, Communication and Leadership Experience

- Strong track record of leading high-quality research, publications, proposals, software tools and experimental campaigns.
- Experience providing technical or matrix leadership across senior multidisciplinary contributors.
- Experience advising and influencing senior stakeholders, programme decisions and capability-investment priorities.
- Recognised internally as an expert in experimental design, validation or a closely related discipline, with an established external profile.
- Proven experience developing senior engineers, researchers and technical leaders and building national or international networks.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management or matrix-management responsibility.
- May hold budgetary authority and responsibility for major projects, facilities or resources.
- Represent the organisation in national and international forums, collaborations and advisory activities.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

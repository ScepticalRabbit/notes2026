# Principal Computational Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Principal Computational Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** I  
**Reports to:** Fusion Technology Group Leader  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

Lead significant computational experimental-design capabilities and major technical workstreams within complex fusion research and engineering programmes. Define technical approaches, computational architectures and validation strategies across computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, verification, validation and uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware computational engineering.

The role is accountable for delivering robust, reusable and high-impact computational capability that improves the information gained from validation experiments and supports risk-aware engineering decisions. By improving the effective use of experimental facilities, materials, time and funding, the role contributes to sustainable and commercially viable fusion energy and creates UK economic benefit through deployment into fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Define and lead significant technical workstreams and computational experimental design capabilities within major, complex research and engineering programmes, establishing technical objectives, delivery approaches and measures of success.
- Hold delegated technical authority for the selection, adaptation and development of mathematical methods, computational architectures and validation strategies within assigned areas of responsibility.
- Design and oversee integrated toolboxes of interoperating computational libraries that combine performant compiled implementations with accessible Python interfaces for use by engineers, experimentalists and researchers.
- Lead the development and research of new algorithms, sensor simulation models, validation metrics and uncertainty quantification methods where established approaches are insufficient for the engineering need.
- Establish and assure technical standards for performance, numerical correctness, verification, reproducibility, interoperability, documentation, deployment and maintainability across assigned programmes and capabilities.
- Define and coordinate simulation, software-verification and experimental-validation activities required to demonstrate that computational and experimental analysis methods are fit for their intended engineering use.
- Provide authoritative technical review and approval for significant methods, architectures, software releases and validation approaches within delegated areas of responsibility.
- Lead multidisciplinary contributors across experiments, simulation, measurement science, applied mathematics, statistics and high-performance computing, resolving technical interdependencies and conflicting requirements.
- Plan significant packages of work and influence the allocation of people, budgets, computing resources and experimental activity, exercising delegated control where assigned and managing technical and programme risk.
- Originate and lead research projects, major proposals, peer-reviewed publications and external/internal collaborations, while contributing to the development of larger strategic programmes.
- Build partnerships with universities, industry and research organisations to extend capability, attract investment and accelerate adoption and impact.
- Translate computational methods and design tools into practical use within fusion programmes and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, delivering scientific, industrial and UK economic benefit.
- Influence organisational strategy, investment priorities and policy in computational experimental design; verification, validation and uncertainty quantificagtion; and performance-aware computational engineering.
- Mentor and technically lead senior engineers and researchers, develop capability across the job family and support succession within the discipline.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- Extensive and broad experience in computational research and engineering. A doctorate or equivalent record of original research and technical leadership may be advantageous.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Deep knowledge of Python-based scientific computing and the design of accessible interfaces to performant compiled implementations.
- Advanced knowledge of one or more systems programming languages and of the architectural decisions required to deliver high-performance numerical software across modern processors or accelerator platforms.
- Demonstrable expertise in performance-aware computational engineering, including data-oriented design, memory hierarchy, vectorisation, parallelism, accelerator computing, numerical efficiency and reproducible benchmarking.
- Proven ability to define computational architectures, technical standards and development practices for complex, interoperating scientific libraries and toolboxes.
- Proven ability to assess and assure the correctness, performance, robustness and maintainability of computational methods developed by multidisciplinary teams.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Advanced knowledge in computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, verification, validation and uncertainty quantification, or a closely related field, with broad capability across the wider discipline.
- Proven ability to select, adapt and develop mathematical and numerical methods for complex engineering problems where objectives are broad and precedent may be limited.
- Extensive experience defining and developing verification, validation and uncertainty quantification strategies that support significant engineering or programme decisions.
- Ability to evaluate the adequacy, limitations and risks of computational methods and provide authoritative technical recommendations and approvals within delegated areas.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- Recognised expertise in at least two of the following areas, with substantial breadth across the others: simulation-driven or Bayesian experimental design; sensor, imaging or measurement-system modelling; measurement uncertainty and uncertainty propagation; mathematical methods for verification, validation and uncertainty quantification; numerical optimisation, statistical inference or information-theoretic methods; image-based measurement, computational imaging or synthetic data generation; or performance-aware numerical and scientific computing.
- Demonstrable experience integrating multiple specialist areas into a coherent computational capability.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- A strong track record of leading high-quality research, peer-reviewed publications, conference outputs and collaborative technical programmes.
- Demonstrable experience originating and leading research projects, grant proposals and national or international collaborations.
- Ability to communicate complex scientific and engineering issues to specialist and non-specialist audiences and influence senior stakeholders across disciplinary boundaries.
- Experience translating computational methods or engineering tools into operational use and demonstrable scientific, engineering or industrial impact.
- Proven ability to mentor senior engineers and researchers, coordinate multidisciplinary contributors and build sustainable technical capability.

## Additional Duties

- Represent the organisation in national and international technical forums, conferences, collaborations and advisory activities relevant to the role.
- Undertake national and international travel where required to lead collaborative programmes, research partnerships and technical engagements.
- May hold budgetary authority and may line-manage or matrix-manage engineers, researchers, students, contractors or collaborators.
- Contribute to recruitment, capability planning, technical assurance and succession across the Computational Experimental Design Engineer job family.
- Complete and maintain any appointments, training or professional requirements necessary to discharge delegated technical and organisational responsibilities.

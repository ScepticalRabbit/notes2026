# Proposed Job Description: Senior Fellow in Fusion Technology Validation

**Verification, validation, uncertainty quantification and simulation-driven experimental design**

## Role Summary

**Role title:** Senior Fellow in Fusion Technology Validation  
**Job family:** Scientific  
**Grade:** K  
**Direct reports:** None required  
**Primary focus:** Technical and scientific leadership in verification, validation, uncertainty quantification (VVUQ) and simulation-driven experimental design for fusion technologies.

## Overall Purpose

Provide organisation-wide scientific direction and leadership in fusion technology validation.

The role will establish and lead an internationally recognised programme integrating experimentation, high-fidelity simulation, research software, verification, validation and uncertainty quantification. Its purpose is to ensure that decisions about fusion technologies are supported by credible, traceable and proportionate evidence.

The Senior Fellow will define how simulation-driven experimental design, synthetic experimental data and AI-enabled optimisation can be used to improve the value of scarce and expensive experiments. The role will connect experimental programmes with UKAEA's high-performance computing and AI capabilities, including the Sunrise AI supercomputer, and translate those assets into practical qualification methods.

The role will act as the organisation's principal scientific authority for VVUQ and fusion technology validation, providing independent scrutiny, challenge and advice to major programmes and senior leadership.

## Strategic Context

Fusion technologies must be developed and qualified under conditions where experimental evidence is limited, testing is costly, models are uncertain and direct replication of service conditions may be impossible.

UKAEA therefore requires a coherent fusion technology validation capability spanning:

- experimental mechanics and materials testing;
- computational modelling and simulation;
- verification, validation and uncertainty quantification;
- high-performance scientific software;
- synthetic experimental data;
- simulation-driven experimental design;
- machine-learning-enabled optimisation;
- evidence synthesis for engineering decisions.

The role will provide the technical vision that joins these disciplines into a sustained organisational capability for fusion technology validation.

Pyvale and its associated software ecosystem will be a major enabling platform, but the role is broader than ownership of a software package. Its purpose is to define and lead the scientific framework by which experiments, simulations and uncertainty evidence are combined to validate fusion technologies and support component qualification.

## Principal Accountabilities

### Scientific leadership

- Lead and direct an internationally recognised programme of research and innovation in fusion technology validation and VVUQ.
- Define the long-term scientific vision for simulation-driven experimental design and evidence-based validation of fusion technologies.
- Establish new research questions, methods and technical directions that improve the credibility, efficiency and value of fusion-component testing.
- Maintain substantial personal scientific output with demonstrable impact, comparable with that expected from a professor at an internationally leading university.
- Publish in high-quality journals, present at major international conferences and give invited lectures and seminars.

### Organisational strategy and authority

- Design and communicate an organisation-wide strategy for fusion technology validation and VVUQ.
- Act as the senior scientific authority and principal point of consultation for complex verification, validation, uncertainty and qualification problems.
- Scrutinise, challenge and advise major scientific and engineering programmes on the adequacy of their experimental evidence, modelling assumptions, validation strategies and treatment of uncertainty.
- Provide authoritative recommendations to senior leadership where qualification risk, evidence quality or model credibility could affect programme decisions.
- Shape organisational investment priorities in experimental design, scientific software, AI, high-performance computing and validation capability.

### Research programme development

- Initiate, develop and lead major externally and internally funded research programmes.
- Build a sustainable portfolio of grants, strategic collaborations, co-funded studentships and long-term technical partnerships.
- Develop research themes that persist beyond individual grants and become embedded organisational capabilities.
- Create a self-sustaining pipeline of technical leaders, researchers and grant applicants.

### Simulation-driven experimental design

- Establish simulation-driven experimental design as a core capability for fusion engineering.
- Lead the development of methods that determine which experiments should be performed, at what fidelity, with which measurements, and for what expected reduction in uncertainty.
- Use synthetic experimental data, high-fidelity simulation and machine learning to optimise experimental campaigns.
- Ensure that Sunrise and other high-performance computing assets are exploited in ways that directly support experimental planning, component validation and qualification decisions.

### Software and computational capability

- Lead the scientific direction of Pyvale and related research-software capabilities for fusion technology validation.
- Ensure that software methods are scientifically credible, computationally efficient, open where appropriate, and suitable for use across large-scale organisational programmes.
- Promote advanced computational approaches, including high-performance computing, accelerator technologies, data-oriented design and AI-enabled optimisation.
- Ensure that enabling software is integrated with experimental, modelling and qualification workflows rather than developed as an isolated research product.

### Collaboration and external representation

- Build and maintain strong collaborations with UK universities, national laboratories, research and technology organisations, industry and international partners.
- Represent UKAEA on national and international committees, panels, professional bodies and scientific collaborations relevant to fusion technology validation, VVUQ and experimental mechanics.
- Supervise PhD students and support postdoctoral researchers and technical specialists.
- Develop the external standing of UKAEA in qualification science and establish the organisation as a recognised leader in the field.

### Capability building and technical leadership

- Mentor and develop technical specialists, research software engineers, experimentalists and early-career researchers.
- Enable staff to lead their own research themes, publications, collaborations and external funding proposals.
- Coordinate technical work across disciplines and organisational boundaries without requiring formal line-management responsibility.
- Build succession and resilience so that the capability is sustained beyond the tenure of any individual grant or fellowship.

## Scope and Authority

The role will operate with substantial scientific autonomy and organisation-wide influence.

It will:

- set technical direction within fusion technology validation and VVUQ;
- define research priorities and methods;
- provide final or authoritative scientific recommendations within its field;
- influence cross-divisional strategy and major programme decisions;
- coordinate work across multiple disciplines and external organisations;
- challenge existing approaches where the scientific evidence is inadequate;
- develop new policies, frameworks and operating methods where precedent is limited.

Formal line management is not essential to the role. Its authority derives from recognised expertise, strategic responsibility, scientific leadership and organisational impact.

## Expected Qualifications and Experience

The post holder will normally demonstrate:

- an internationally recognised record of research in technology validation, VVUQ, experimental mechanics, computational modelling, scientific software or closely related fields;
- substantial personal publication output and evidence of sustained scientific impact;
- a proven ability to originate and lead multi-year research programmes;
- successful leadership of externally funded grants or fellowships;
- experience supervising PhD students and developing early-career researchers;
- recognised expertise across experimentation, simulation and software;
- evidence of influencing organisational or programme strategy;
- strong national and international collaborations;
- invited lectures, conference presentations, committee service or equivalent external recognition;
- a track record of creating methods, software or capabilities adopted beyond the originating team;
- the ability to advise and challenge senior stakeholders on complex scientific matters.

## Indicators of Success

Evidence that the role is operating successfully would include:

- adoption of fusion technology validation methods across multiple UKAEA programmes and divisions;
- formal dependence of internal and external programmes on Pyvale or related capabilities;
- measurable influence on experimental plans, validation strategies and qualification decisions;
- sustained high-quality publication output from both the Senior Fellow and the wider team;
- externally funded research programmes and a strong pipeline of bids;
- multiple PhD students, university collaborations and independent technical leaders developed through the programme;
- recognised national and international standing in VVUQ and fusion technology validation;
- effective exploitation of Sunrise and other computing assets for experimental design and qualification;
- integration of the capability into long-term organisational strategy beyond the current fellowship.

## Relationship to Existing Senior Fellow Precedent

This proposed role follows the same underlying Grade K model as the existing Senior Fellow role in nuclear science and technology:

- organisation-wide scientific leadership;
- development of an internationally recognised research programme;
- professor-comparable personal scientific output;
- creation of new methods and technical capabilities;
- influence over organisational strategy;
- leadership of university and international collaborations;
- authoritative scrutiny and challenge of major scientific programmes;
- substantial technical autonomy without requiring conventional line management.

The distinctive focus of this role is Fusion Technology Validation: the integration of experimentation, simulation, VVUQ, AI and scientific software to establish credible evidence for the design, development and qualification of fusion technologies.

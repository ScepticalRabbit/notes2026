# Senior Fellow in Computational Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Senior Fellow in Computational Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** K  
**Reports to:** [Insert job title]  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** Not required  

## Overall Purpose

Provide scientific direction and international leadership in computational experimental design, shaping the organisation's long-term strategy across simulation-driven experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware computational engineering. Establish new research directions and ensure that state-of-the-art scientific and computational approaches are used to develop and deliver solutions for fusion engineering.

Through internationally recognised research, strategic partnerships and authoritative scientific challenge, position the organisation at the forefront of experimental design and validation for fusion. Create sustained scientific, industrial and UK economic benefit by enabling more informative, resource-efficient validation programmes and transferring methods and computational technologies into adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

A Senior Fellow in Computational Experimental Design provides internationally recognised scientific leadership, establishing new research directions and shaping the organisation's long-term strategy and external standing in the discipline.

## Accountabilities

- Define and lead an internationally recognised programme of research and innovation in computational experimental design and associated validation sciences.
- Provide scientific direction across the organisation, shaping long-term strategy and advising senior leadership on priorities and investment.
- Establish new research fields, methods and experimental approaches where there is little or no precedent.
- Lead major integrated computational and experimental capabilities spanning simulation-driven design, measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware scientific computing.
- Scrutinise, challenge and advise on major organisational scientific and engineering programmes.
- Influence or establish organisation-wide scientific standards, policies and strategic practice in the discipline.
- Initiate and lead major national and international collaborations with universities, industry, research organisations and government bodies.
- Produce major personal scientific output with international impact, including influential publications, invited presentations and leadership of internationally significant programmes.
- Represent the organisation at the highest national and international levels.
- Shape significant organisational and external investment in future scientific capability.
- Lead translation and exploitation across fusion and adjacent high-value sectors.
- Develop Principal and other senior technical leaders and act as the senior source of scientific authority and challenge.
- Use the enhanced freedom and status of a Senior Fellow to pursue new lines of thought aligned with the organisation's mission.

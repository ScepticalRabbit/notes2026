# Principal Computational Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Principal Computational Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** I  
**Reports to:** [Insert job title]  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

Lead a major computational capability or programme area for the design, simulation and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. Provide strategic technical leadership across computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware computational engineering within broad organisational and programme objectives.

The role holds delegated authority for significant multidisciplinary outcomes and influences organisational strategy, investment and technical direction. It improves the information gained from major validation programmes, enables more efficient use of facilities and resources, and supports sustainable and commercially viable fusion energy with wider UK economic benefit.

A Principal Computational Experimental Design Engineer owns a major computational capability or programme area, providing strategic technical leadership and delegated authority for significant multidisciplinary research and engineering outcomes.

## Accountabilities

- Define and lead major computational capabilities or programme areas within complex research and engineering portfolios.
- Establish technical direction, roadmaps and assurance approaches within broad organisational objectives.
- Hold delegated authority for significant mathematical methods, computational architectures and validation strategies.
- Oversee integrated toolboxes of interoperating computational libraries and associated experimental-validation programmes.
- Sponsor and direct development of new methods and algorithms where existing approaches are inadequate.
- Provide final technical review and approval within assigned programmes or delegated areas.
- Lead major multidisciplinary collaborations and coordinate senior technical contributors across projects and organisations.
- Influence allocation of people, budgets, computing resources and experimental activity, exercising delegated authority where assigned.
- Originate and lead substantial research proposals, programmes, publications and external partnerships.
- Influence organisational strategy, policy and investment priorities in the discipline.
- Translate computational methods and tools into strategic use across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.
- Develop technical leaders and build sustainable capability and succession across the job family.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant quantitative discipline.
- Extensive and broad experience in computational research and engineering. A doctorate or equivalent record of original research and technical leadership may be advantageous.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Deep knowledge of scientific computing, systems programming and accessible interfaces to performant compiled implementations.
- Demonstrable expertise in performance-aware computational architecture across modern CPU and accelerator platforms.
- Proven ability to define technical standards and architectures for complex, interoperating scientific libraries and toolboxes.
- Experience providing authoritative assurance of correctness, robustness, performance and maintainability.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Deep expertise in at least one core capability area and advanced breadth across the wider discipline.
- Proven ability to select, adapt and develop methods for complex problems where objectives are broad and precedent is limited.
- Extensive experience establishing validation and uncertainty strategies for significant engineering or programme decisions.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- Recognised internal and external expertise in computational experimental design or a closely related discipline.
- Experience integrating several specialist areas into a major capability, toolbox or programme.
- Ability to act as a senior technical approver within delegated areas.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- A sustained track record of leading high-quality research, publications, proposals and major collaborations.
- Experience translating research into operational engineering use and demonstrable scientific or industrial impact.
- Ability to influence senior stakeholders and organisational decision-making.
- Proven experience developing senior engineers, researchers and technical leaders.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management or matrix-management responsibility.
- May hold delegated budgetary authority and responsibility for major programmes, facilities or resources.
- Represent the organisation in national and international forums, collaborations and advisory activities.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

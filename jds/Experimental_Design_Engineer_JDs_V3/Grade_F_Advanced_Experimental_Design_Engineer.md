# Advanced Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Advanced Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** F  
**Reports to:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer or Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

An Advanced Experimental Design Engineer delivers complete computational, experimental or integrated studies by independently selecting and adapting established methods for moderately complex and variable engineering problems.

The purpose of this role is to independently deliver complete studies that improve the quality, efficiency and credibility of fusion engineering validation. The role develops and applies computational methods, measurement approaches and practical experimental workflows across sensor and measurement modelling, instrumentation, data acquisition, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware scientific computing.

By connecting predictive models with traceable measurements, the role delivers decision-ready evidence for project teams, reduces repeated effort and unnecessary testing, and supports translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **strong technical knowledge** across computational and experimental methods to deliver complete studies, producing reliable and reusable outputs for project teams, test engineers and internal users.
- Independently select and adapt established methods for **moderately complex and variable** problems where precedent requires interpretation, resolving routine departures within defined objectives.
- Deliver a complete defined **study** by planning study resources, selecting methods and coordinating assigned computing, equipment, facility time and contributors to achieve agreed technical outcomes.
- Convert defined engineering objectives into complete experimental-design, modelling, validation and uncertainty workflows that provide decision-ready evidence for project stakeholders.
- Develop robust computational implementations and accessible interfaces that allow internal users to apply specialist methods consistently and efficiently.
- Profile and improve analysis workflows, reducing runtime, memory use and repeated manual effort across the study and related project activities.
- Plan and deliver experimental activities, selecting sensors, instrumentation layouts and acquisition settings that produce reliable measurements for the study.
- Establish calibration, synchronisation and measurement-chain traceability so that test engineers and analysts understand the quality and limitations of acquired data.
- Process and interpret experimental data independently, diagnosing common instrumentation and data-quality problems and implementing corrective actions within study scope.
- Develop verification, validation and uncertainty workflows that improve reproducibility and technical confidence in study conclusions.
- Compare simulations and measurements using appropriate metrics and acceptance criteria, producing clear recommendations for project engineers.
- Produce complete reports, software documentation, experimental records and publication-quality outputs that enable review, reuse and dissemination.
- Advise project colleagues and present technical work at internal and external meetings, adapting communication to secure agreement on study methods, findings and next steps.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- A relevant doctorate, or equivalent engineering research experience gained within a structured research, industrial or technical environment.
- **Strong core knowledge** of applied experimental design, including the relationship between engineering questions, computational models, measurements, uncertainty, evidence quality and decision needs.
- **Established experience** independently delivering complete studies involving moderately complex and variable computational, experimental or integrated work.
- **Strong knowledge and established experience** in one or more advanced experimental-design skills, such as scientific Python, numerical analysis, compiled or systems programming, profiling and optimisation, sensors and instrumentation, data-acquisition architecture, advanced diagnostics, signal processing, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Growing breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to integrate digital and physical evidence without routine supervision.
- **Strong knowledge** of relevant engineering mathematics, numerical methods and physical-modelling principles used to interpret simulation and experimental results.
- **Strong knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing at scripting-language level, including profiling, algorithmic efficiency, memory use and routes to higher-performance implementation.
- **Established experience** designing reproducible computational or experimental workflows using testing, documentation, version control, calibration records or structured data management.
- **Established experience** planning and using study resources, including computing, equipment, facility time or contributions from others.
- **Established experience** comparing simulation and measurement outputs and defining suitable metrics, uncertainty estimates and acceptance criteria.
- **Broader experience** communicating technical work through reports, publications, presentations, software documentation or experimental records.
- **Established capability** to resolve moderately complex technical problems independently and escalate issues with consequences beyond the study.

### Desirable

- **Growing specialist knowledge** in a second advanced area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental route.
- **Growing breadth** through experience of both advanced performance engineering and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Broader external or collaborative experience** contributing to research proposals, reusable software, publications or multi-partner experimental programmes.

## Additional Duties

- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- May supervise students, placements or discrete tasks undertaken by less experienced colleagues.
- May hold delegated responsibility for defined study budgets or resources.

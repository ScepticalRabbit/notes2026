# 📑 OpenOffice Copy Template: Grade I

**Role Title:** Principal Computational Performance Architect

**Division / Function:** Research & Development / Fusion Technology

**Grade:** Grade I

**Reports to:** Research Group Lead

**Skill Type:** Strategic Organisational & Research Authority

**Line Manager:** Yes

### Overall Purpose

This role exists as a definitive organisational authority to invent, architect, and deliver next-generation computational frameworks that solve the most critical multi-physics verification, validation, and uncertainty quantification problems in fusion engineering. It aims to formulate broad technical policies, secure institutional-scale research grants, and dictate long-term computing strategies, positioning the organisation as an international leader in simulation-driven experimental design and sensor-based Uncertainty Quantification (UQ). Without this role, the organisation would be bounded by commercial or legacy software limitations, halting the development of large-scale virtual testing loops necessary to de-risk high-value fusion infrastructure.

### Accountabilities

* **Influence Strategic Direction:** Evaluate, recommend, and contribute substantively to both the research group strategy and the wider division strategy, aligning high-performance computing goals with long-term functional objectives.


* **Architect Institutional Toolchains:** Provide technical strategic leadership in performance-aware programming methodologies, establishing the data-oriented design patterns, cache/memory coherence layouts, and hardware-aware parallel multi-threading frameworks needed to deliver high-fidelity sensor UQ tools.


* **Resolve Complex Situations:** Identify, define, and analyse complex situations requiring innovative solutions where there is limited technical precedent, employing sophisticated analytical, interpretative, and evaluative thinking to develop multiple viable computing paths.


* **Execute Final Project Sign-Off:** Exercise the formal authority to act as the final technical sign-off and senior point of approval on complex computing projects, virtual qualification protocols, and multi-library cross-language software pipelines.


* **Secure Research Funding:** Proactively identify internal and external funding mechanisms, draft complex technical proposals, and secure external research grants to expand the department’s computational performance capabilities.


* **Lead Research Dissemination:** Oversee high-impact research outputs by authoring definitive, peer-reviewed journal articles, presenting technical breakthroughs at major international conferences, and maintaining links with external professional networks and universities.


* **Manage Personnel & Resources:** Execute line management and matrix management responsibilities for technical specialists, overseeing task allocation, project resource distribution, and contributing directly to formal performance review cycles.



### Specific Qualifications/Experience

* **Minimum Qualifications:** Seasoned professional holding a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in an engineering, computer science, or highly quantitative mathematical discipline, or equivalent deep, broad, and comprehensive work experience, combined with active Chartered Engineer status.


* **Technical Mastery:** Recognised internally and externally as an industry expert in low-level systems compilation, hardware topology optimization, and high-performance cross-language interface design spanning systems programming languages and accessible Python scripting environments.


* **Programmatic Know-How:** Extensive experience managing complex technical projects, managing multi-year research budgets, and steering risk management matrices within assigned work packages.


* **Soft Skills:** Outstanding communication skills, professional judgement in navigating complex organizational interdependencies, and a proven track record of building consensus among different engineering disciplines.



### Additional Duties

* Serve on relevant national or international technical advisory bodies, steering committees, or peer-review funding boards as an institutional representative.


* Act as an organisation-wide mentor, actively developing team capabilities, identifying talent, and mapping out critical succession plans.


* Promote and champion the organisation's values, equity, diversity, and inclusion directives, while enforcing behaviours that support the safety, health, and welfare of all colleagues.

# Lead Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** H  
**Reports to:** Principal Experimental Design Engineer or Head of Experimental Design  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Lead Experimental Design Engineer leads major projects or defined capability areas, sets integrated technical direction and is accountable for coordinated delivery through technical or matrix leadership.

The purpose of this role is to lead integrated projects  that combine advanced computation, physical experimentation and measurement science to resolve complex fusion engineering challenges. The role sets technical direction across substantial bodies of work spanning computational experimental design, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, data acquisition and performance-aware computing. It coordinates multidisciplinary contributors, establishes assurance approaches and develops methods for problems where precedent is limited.

By creating coherent computational-experimental evidence, the role strengthens major engineering decisions, improves the use of specialist facilities and accelerates deployment of new methods and technologies into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors. 

## Accountabilities

- Define and lead projects, groups of related substanstial work packages or a defined experimental design capability area.
- Establish integrated technical approaches across simulation, sensor and measurement modelling, practical test design, data acquisition, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification and performance-aware computing.
- Set technical objectives, standards, delivery structures, interfaces and assurance requirements within assigned programmes or capability areas.
- Direct the development of new computational and experimental methods for complex problems with limited precedent.
- Establish validation, uncertainty, calibration and data-quality strategies for significant projects.
- Lead the design and delivery of complex experimental programmes involving multiple diagnostics, facilities, teams or operational constraints.
- Coordinate architectures and interfaces across software libraries, analysis workflows, instrumentation systems and experimental datasets.
- Act as a senior technical reviewer and delegated approver for significant methods, plans, results and evidence packages.
- Provide technical or matrix leadership to multidisciplinary teams, resolving conflicts and coordinating senior contributors.
- Influence programme planning, resource allocation, facility use and investment within assigned areas.
- Lead major external collaborations, proposals, publications, software capabilities and experimental datasets.
- Develop technical leaders, mentor staff and contribute to succession and capability planning.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive relevant experience or a doctorate supported by a strong record of technical, research and project leadership.
- Demonstrable experience leading major multidisciplinary computational and experimental activity.

### Computational Knowledge and Experience

- Deep knowledge of scientific computing, numerical software, compiled systems programming languages and performance-aware computational architecture.
- Proven ability to define technical standards and architectures across multiple libraries, workflows, projects or contributors.
- Strong understanding of modern central processing unit (CPU), vector, parallel and accelerator platforms and their implications for scientific software design.
- Experience assuring correctness, scalability, interoperability, usability and maintainability across substantial computational capabilities.
- Ability to direct development of new algorithms and software approaches and integrate performant implementations with accessible engineering workflows.

### Experimental Knowledge and Experience

- Deep practical understanding of experimental design, instrumentation, measurement systems and test delivery.
- Proven ability to define and lead complex experimental campaigns involving multiple sensors, diagnostics, facilities or environmental constraints.
- Strong knowledge of calibration, synchronisation, traceability, measurement uncertainty and data-quality assurance.
- Experience reviewing and approving instrumentation strategies, diagnostic selections, acquisition architectures and test-readiness evidence.
- Advanced knowledge of one or more specialist diagnostic areas, with sufficient breadth to integrate unfamiliar techniques through expert teams.

### Engineering Analysis and Validation Knowledge and Experience

- Deep expertise in at least one core capability area of experimental design, measurement modelling, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, numerical simulation and strong breadth across the wider discipline.
- Proven ability to select, adapt and develop methods for complex, multidisciplinary problems with limited precedent.
- Experience establishing validation and uncertainty strategies for engineering decisions.
- Ability to connect experimental design with model credibility, decision risk, information value and resource constraints.
- Strong judgement in balancing technical ambition, practical feasibility, cost, schedule and evidence quality.

### Research, Communication and Leadership Experience

- Track record of leading major research or engineering outputs, publications, proposals, software tools and experimental programmes.
- Experience providing technical or matrix leadership across multidisciplinary teams.
- Ability to influence senior technical stakeholders and build consensus across projects and disciplines.
- Recognised as a subject-matter expert internally and increasingly externally.
- Experience developing engineers, researchers and technical leaders and maintaining strong national or international networks.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management and/or matrix-management responsibility for a multidisciplinary team.
- May hold delegated authority for substantial budgets, resources, facilities and technical approvals.
- Represent the organisation in national and international technical forums and collaborations.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

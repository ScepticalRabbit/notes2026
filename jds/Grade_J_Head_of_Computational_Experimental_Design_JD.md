# Head of Computational Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Head of Computational Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** J  
**Reports to:** [Insert job title]  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

Direct a portfolio of computational experimental-design capabilities and programmes across the organisation, setting cross-cutting technical direction and coordinating Principal-level leaders. Shape organisational strategy, investment and partnerships across computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware computational engineering.

The role ensures that major programmes use coherent, state-of-the-art methods and architectures and that investment is directed toward capabilities needed for future fusion delivery. It creates organisation-wide scientific, engineering and UK economic impact through strategic deployment and external translation of computational technologies.

A Head of Computational Experimental Design directs a portfolio of computational capabilities and programmes, setting cross-cutting technical direction and shaping organisational strategy, investment and partnerships in the discipline.

## Accountabilities

- Direct the organisation's portfolio of computational experimental-design capabilities, programmes and technical leaders.
- Set cross-programme technical direction, architecture, assurance and investment priorities.
- Approve or sponsor critical methods, architectures and capability developments with significant organisational impact.
- Lead policy and strategy development in the discipline and influence wider organisational priorities.
- Coordinate allocation and reallocation of substantial resources across programmes and capability areas.
- Sponsor major research programmes, strategic partnerships and national or international funding initiatives.
- Provide authoritative recommendations to senior leadership on technical risk, programme credibility and future capability.
- Build and maintain major collaborations with government, universities, industry and international research organisations.
- Develop Principal-level leaders, succession plans and organisational capability across the discipline.
- Represent the organisation in senior national and international forums and strategic partnerships.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant quantitative discipline.
- Extensive, broad and comprehensive experience across computational research, engineering and organisational leadership.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of original research and technical leadership would normally be expected.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Broad authority across advanced computational methods, architectures and assurance for major scientific and engineering programmes.
- Proven ability to direct technical strategy across multiple programmes, platforms and organisational units.
- Experience approving critical computational methods and investments with organisation-wide consequences.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Recognised external expertise across computational experimental design and related validation sciences.
- Proven ability to direct multidisciplinary approaches to complex and unprecedented engineering problems.
- Experience shaping organisational policy, strategy and investment in technical capability.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- Deep expertise in one or more core capability areas with broad authority across the discipline.
- Experience integrating multiple programmes and technical leaders into a coherent organisational capability.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- A strong national and international profile with a sustained record of major research leadership and impact.
- Proven ability to lead large-scale programmes, strategic funding initiatives and external partnerships.
- Experience influencing senior organisational, government, industrial and academic stakeholders.
- Demonstrable success developing senior technical leaders and sustainable organisational capability.

## Additional Duties

- May lead a substantial function, portfolio or network of technical teams.

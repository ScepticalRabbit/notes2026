# Senior Fellow in Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Senior Fellow in Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** K  
**Reports to:** Director or higher  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Senior Fellow in Experimental Design is an internationally recognised authority and organisation-wide go-to person who shapes the direction and development of a field, creating new research agendas, methods and communities of practice.

The purpose of this role is to establish new fields and shape the organisation’s long-term technical direction, external standing and impact in integrated computational and experimental validation. The role operates as an organisation-level authority across advanced diagnostics, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, performance-aware computing and evidence-led engineering.

By defining the state of the art and translating it into sustainable organisational and industrial capability, the role advances fusion engineering, strengthens national and international scientific leadership and creates long-term value for the wider field.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **internationally recognised authority** as the organisation-wide go-to person for in-scope problems, providing specialist advice that shapes new scientific and engineering capability for the organisation and wider field.
- Originate approaches for **unprecedented, field-defining and strategically critical** challenges where little or no precedent exists nationally or internationally.
- Shape a **field** by providing organisation-level authoritative judgement and influencing major strategic investment, external agendas and long-term capability direction.
- Establish new methods, diagnostics, software architectures, validation frameworks or experimental paradigms that enable new classes of engineering evidence.
- Provide technical direction with a strong impact on organisational strategic thinking and future direction, while shaping national and international research agendas.
- Sponsor and guide major programmes, capabilities and senior technical leaders across organisations and sectors around field-shaping objectives.
- Provide authoritative scrutiny and challenge to strategically critical programmes and evidence strategies, protecting scientific, engineering and reputational outcomes.
- Originate major national and international partnerships, funding initiatives, advisory activity and research networks that expand the field.
- Represent the organisation at the highest technical levels with industry, academia, funders and national or international bodies, shaping standards and collaborative agendas.
- Influence strategic investment in facilities, software, diagnostics, people and partnerships without necessarily holding routine operational ownership.
- Translate frontier research into sustained adoption across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, creating long-term scientific and economic impact.
- Shape agendas and persuade senior authorities and international peers to pursue new directions in research, engineering and capability development.
- Represent the organisation on relevant national and international advisory bodies, shaping external research agendas, standards and strategic priorities within the field.
- Develop Principal-level staff, future Fellows and emerging international leaders, strengthening succession within the organisation and wider discipline.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of sustained original achievement would normally be expected.
- **Internationally recognised authority** in experimental design or a relevant computational, experimental, measurement or validation specialist field.
- **Distinguished and sustained experience** shaping a field and addressing unprecedented, field-defining challenges with little or no precedent.
- **Field-shaping knowledge and distinguished experience** in one or more specialist areas, such as advanced scientific computing, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement science, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **National and international breadth** sufficient to connect the specialist field with complementary computational and experimental disciplines and shape integrated agendas.
- **Internationally recognised knowledge** of performance-aware computing or its strategic relevance to scientific and engineering capability.
- **Field-shaping knowledge** of evidence, uncertainty, model credibility, measurement assurance and risk in unprecedented engineering decisions.
- **Distinguished and sustained experience** establishing new frameworks, standards, principles or capability adopted beyond a single programme or organisation.
- **National and international experience** influencing strategic investment, research agendas and partnerships through technical authority.
- **Distinguished and sustained experience** influencing senior leaders, external institutions and international peers through major programmes, publications, advisory roles and partnerships.
- **Distinguished and sustained experience** serving as an organisation-wide go-to authority, advising senior leaders and representing an organisation on national or international advisory bodies.
- **National and international experience** creating new research directions and influencing the strategic thinking and future direction of organisations and the wider field.
- **Proven experience** developing Principal-level staff, Fellows and future international leaders through technical authority and matrix influence.

### Desirable

- **Field-shaping specialist knowledge** extending into a complementary computational or experimental route beyond the role holder’s primary authority.
- **National and international breadth** spanning both advanced scientific computing and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Distinguished external experience** shaping standards, research agendas or major collaborative programmes.
- Fellowship of an appropriate professional or learned body, chartered status or equivalent international standing where relevant.

## Additional Duties

- May lead a substantial function, portfolio or network of technical teams, but line management is not a requirement.
- May hold authority over major resources, organisational approvals or strategic appointments.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

# Principal Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Principal Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** I  
**Reports to:** Head of Experimental Design or equivalent senior leader  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Principal Experimental Design Engineer is an industry-recognised subject-matter expert who leads and is accountable for one significant computational, experimental or integrated capability, exercising authoritative judgement on strategically important problems with limited or emerging precedent.

The purpose of this role is to lead a significant experimental-design capability within a broader research and engineering portfolio. The role integrates computation, advanced diagnostics and practical experimentation across sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, high-performance scientific computing and test delivery.

By establishing sustainable capability and authoritative evidence, the role improves major programme decisions, prioritises scarce resources effectively and accelerates translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **authoritative technical knowledge** as an industry-recognised subject-matter expert, providing authoritative specialist advice and trusted direction to divisional stakeholders, programme sponsors and senior technical authorities.
- Establish and shape approaches for **significant, complex and strategically important** problems where precedent is limited or emerging and novel integration is often required.
- Lead and be accountable for a **capability** by approving significant technical decisions and prioritising substantial people, budgets, facilities, computing and experimental resources against a multiyear roadmap.
- Establish integrated computational and experimental strategies that align capability outputs with programme evidence needs and organisational priorities.
- Authorise significant methods, architectures, diagnostics and validation approaches within delegated capability scope, improving assurance across major projects.
- Sponsor development of new algorithms, software, diagnostics and test methods where existing capability is inadequate, creating reusable organisational assets.
- Coordinate senior computational, experimental and domain specialists across projects and partner organisations, resolving interfaces affecting major outcomes.
- Hold final technical sign-off within delegated capability scope for complex methods, plans and evidence outputs, escalating decisions with consequences beyond that authority.
- Plan and prioritise capability resources, partnerships and infrastructure to improve delivery, resilience and long-term sustainability.
- Lead substantial proposals, publications, software platforms, datasets and external partnerships that strengthen capability, funding and organisational reputation.
- Advise divisional strategy and investment planning using authoritative evidence from the capability, influencing programme and infrastructure decisions.
- Translate methods, software and experimental capability into use across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, creating impact beyond immediate projects.
- Formulate and maintain technical policy, standards and operating principles for the capability within broad functional guidelines, ensuring consistent practice across programmes and partner activities.
- Provide authoritative advice to senior leaders, funders and strategic partners, represent the organisation in national or international forums, and develop future technical leaders and succession.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive and broad experience across experimental design, computational research, engineering experimentation or a complementary combination of these.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of original research and technical leadership may be advantageous.
- **Authoritative core knowledge** of applied experimental design and the integration of computational models, physical experiments, measurements, uncertainty and engineering decisions.
- **Sustained and significant experience** leading and being accountable for a capability addressing complex and strategically important problems where precedent is limited or emerging.
- **Authoritative knowledge and sustained experience** in one or more specialist areas, such as advanced scientific software, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement science, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Comprehensive breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to exercise authoritative judgement and lead senior specialists.
- **Authoritative knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing and the strategic routes from efficient scripting to compiled, parallel, distributed or accelerator implementations.
- **Comprehensive knowledge** of model credibility, calibration, traceability, measurement uncertainty and evidence quality for significant programme decisions.
- **Sustained experience** establishing technical standards, assurance approaches and reusable capability across major projects.
- **Integrated and strategic experience** prioritising substantial capability resources and balancing technical ambition, feasibility, cost, schedule, risk and information value.
- **Sustained and significant experience** communicating and defending technical direction through major proposals, publications, reviews, advisory activity and external partnerships.
- **Sustained and significant experience** acting as an industry-recognised expert, providing final technical sign-off, authoritative specialist advice and national or international representation within a significant capability.
- **Proven experience** providing technical or matrix leadership across senior contributors and developing future technical leaders.

### Desirable

- **Comprehensive specialist knowledge** in a second advanced area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental authority.
- **Integrated and strategic breadth** spanning both advanced scientific computing and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Strategic external experience** supporting divisional roadmaps, major investment decisions or national and international collaborations.
- Chartered status or equivalent professional standing where relevant.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management or matrix-management responsibility.
- May hold budgetary authority and responsibility for significant capability resources, facilities or infrastructure.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

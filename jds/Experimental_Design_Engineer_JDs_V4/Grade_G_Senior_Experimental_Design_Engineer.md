# Senior Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** G  
**Reports to:** Lead Experimental Design Engineer or Principal Experimental Design Engineer  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Senior Experimental Design Engineer is a recognised senior specialist who owns the delivery and technical quality of computational, experimental or integrated work packages by defining approaches for complex and multifaceted engineering problems with partial precedent.

The purpose of this role is to define and lead work packages that combine computation, instrumentation and experiment to answer difficult fusion engineering questions. The role owns substantial technical outputs spanning computational experimental design, practical test design, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, data acquisition and performance-aware computing.

By improving the quality, efficiency and traceability of validation evidence, the role reduces technical risk for multidisciplinary projects and strengthens engineering decisions across the fusion programme and partner organisations.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **advanced technical knowledge** as a recognised senior specialist, providing specialist advice and owning the technical quality of substantial work packages with wider project and organisational impact.
- Define and develop approaches for **complex and multifaceted** problems where precedent is partial and established methods may not fully fit, evaluating alternatives and resolving technical interfaces.
- Own delivery of a **work package** by defining scope, approving technical outputs within assigned authority and managing allocated people, budget, computing, equipment and facility resources.
- Translate broad project objectives into measurable requirements, methods, dependencies and acceptance evidence that enable coordinated delivery by contributors.
- Own significant software components, measurement methods, diagnostic workflows or specialist capability components so that users receive reliable and reusable tools.
- Define performance, verification, calibration, interoperability and traceability requirements that reduce inconsistency and technical risk across work-package outputs.
- Lead design and delivery of computational-experimental studies, coordinating specialists to produce safe, timely and decision-ready evidence.
- Approve sensor selections, measurement layouts, acquisition strategies, uncertainty budgets and analysis workflows within delegated work-package scope.
- Diagnose complex discrepancies between prediction and measurement, leading structured investigations that identify model, software, instrumentation or test causes.
- Coordinate computational, experimental and domain specialists through technical, project or matrix leadership, assigning work and resolving cross-disciplinary interfaces.
- Manage work-package risks, schedule and resources, escalating issues that affect wider project objectives or programme commitments.
- Lead reports, publications, software releases, experimental datasets and external collaboration outputs that extend team capability and visibility.
- Assign, coordinate and review the work of contributors, providing specialist advice and training so that work-package outputs meet agreed technical and project requirements.
- Influence multidisciplinary decisions by presenting and defending technical recommendations, building agreement across project stakeholders and external collaborators.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive relevant experience, or a doctorate with demonstrable postdoctoral, industrial or technical-leadership experience.
- **Advanced core knowledge** of applied experimental design, including the integration of engineering decisions, models, measurements, uncertainty and acceptance evidence.
- **Substantial experience** owning work packages involving complex and multifaceted computational, experimental or integrated work where precedent is partial.
- **Advanced knowledge and substantial experience** in one or more specialist areas, such as scientific software, compiled or systems programming, performance optimisation, numerical methods, sensors and instrumentation, data-acquisition systems, advanced diagnostics, measurement modelling, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Broad knowledge** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to lead integrated work and review outputs beyond the role holder’s deepest specialism.
- **Advanced knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing, including profiling, algorithm selection, memory behaviour and vector, parallel or accelerator approaches.
- **Advanced knowledge** of how model form, numerical error, sensor behaviour, calibration, test conditions and uncertainty interact.
- **Substantial experience** establishing reproducible and auditable workflows for multiple users or project teams.
- **Substantial experience** defining and managing allocated work-package resources, risks, dependencies and acceptance criteria.
- **Wide experience** evaluating alternative methods and making technically justified decisions where established approaches do not fully fit.
- **Substantial experience** communicating and defending technical outputs through reports, publications, presentations, software or evidence packages.
- **Substantial experience** acting as a recognised senior specialist, providing advice, assigning and reviewing work, and developing specialist capability in others.
- **Proven experience** providing technical or matrix leadership, coordinating contributors and approving outputs within assigned scope.

### Desirable

- **Broad specialist knowledge** in a second advanced area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental route.
- **Broad multidisciplinary experience** across both advanced scientific computing and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Wide external or collaborative experience** contributing to national or international projects, externally funded proposals or shared technical capabilities.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management responsibility and is expected to provide technical management, task coordination, mentoring and review.
- May hold delegated responsibility for work-package budgets, computing resources, laboratory activity or experimental equipment.
- Undertake national or international travel where required.

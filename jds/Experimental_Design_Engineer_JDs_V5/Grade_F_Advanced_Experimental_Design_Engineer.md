# Advanced Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Advanced Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** F  
**Reports to:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer or Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

An Advanced Experimental Design Engineer is a recognised subject-matter specialist who delivers complete computational, experimental or integrated studies by independently selecting and adapting established methods for moderately complex and variable engineering problems.

The purpose of this role is to independently deliver complete studies that improve the quality, efficiency and credibility of fusion engineering validation. The role develops and applies computational methods, measurement approaches and practical experimental workflows across sensor and measurement modelling, instrumentation, data acquisition, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware scientific computing.

By connecting predictive models with traceable measurements, the role delivers decision-ready evidence for project teams, reduces repeated effort and unnecessary testing, and supports translation of experimental design methods into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **strong technical knowledge** to defined study objectives, providing specialist advice and delivering reliable, reusable computational or experimental outputs for project teams, test engineers and internal users.
- Independently select and adapt established methods for **moderately complex and variable** problems where procedures require interpretation, converting incomplete or variable inputs into justified solutions and escalating only issues beyond study scope.
- **Deliver a complete defined study** by planning methods, resources and contributors, producing agreed evidence, reports and reusable outputs to schedule for project stakeholders.
- Convert engineering objectives and acceptance needs into complete experimental-design, modelling, validation and uncertainty workflows that provide decision-ready evidence for project teams.
- Translate mathematical, software and user requirements into robust computational implementations and accessible interfaces that enable internal users to apply specialist methods consistently.
- Use profiling, benchmarking and scaling evidence to improve study workflows, delivering measurable reductions in runtime, memory use or manual effort for project analysts and software users.
- Convert study objectives into sensor selections, instrumentation layouts and acquisition settings that produce reliable measurements for test engineers and project teams.
- Establish calibration, synchronisation and traceability from equipment specifications and test requirements, producing measurement records with quantified quality and limitations for analysts.
- Convert raw experimental data and diagnostic observations into checked datasets, interpreted results and corrective actions that protect study conclusions for project stakeholders.
- Develop verification, validation and uncertainty workflows from study requirements, producing reproducible evidence and quantified confidence in conclusions for technical reviewers.
- Compare simulation and measurement evidence using selected metrics and acceptance criteria, producing technically justified recommendations for project engineers and work-package leads.
- Convert completed study activity into reports, software documentation, experimental records and publication-quality outputs that support review, reuse and dissemination by internal and external audiences.
- Review defined methods, reports, software outputs and experimental records against study and project requirements, producing approved outputs or clear corrective actions that reduce rework for contributors and stakeholders.
- Convert specialist knowledge into advice, mentoring and presentations that secure agreement on study methods and findings and improve capability among project colleagues and less experienced staff.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- A relevant doctorate, or equivalent engineering research experience gained within a structured research, industrial or technical environment.
- **Strong core knowledge** of applied experimental design, including the relationship between engineering questions, computational models, measurements, uncertainty, evidence quality and decision needs.
- **Established experience** independently delivering complete studies involving moderately complex and variable computational, experimental or integrated work.
- **Strong knowledge and established experience** in one or more advanced experimental-design skills, such as scientific Python, numerical analysis, compiled or systems programming, profiling and optimisation, sensors and instrumentation, data-acquisition architecture, advanced diagnostics, signal processing, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Growing breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to integrate digital and physical evidence without routine supervision.
- **Strong knowledge** of relevant engineering mathematics, numerical methods and physical-modelling principles used to interpret simulation and experimental results.
- **Strong knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing at scripting-language level, including profiling, algorithmic efficiency, memory use and routes to higher-performance implementation.
- **Established experience** designing reproducible computational or experimental workflows using testing, documentation, version control, calibration records or structured data management.
- **Established experience** planning and using study resources, including computing, equipment, facility time or contributions from others.
- **Established experience** comparing simulation and measurement outputs and defining suitable metrics, uncertainty estimates and acceptance criteria.
- **Broader experience** communicating technical work through reports, publications, presentations, software documentation or experimental records.
- **Established experience** acting as a recognised specialist, providing advice, reviewing technical outputs and supporting the development of less experienced colleagues.
- **Established capability** to resolve moderately complex technical problems independently and escalate issues with consequences beyond the study.

### Desirable

- **Growing specialist knowledge** in a second advanced area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental route.
- **Growing breadth** through experience of both advanced performance engineering and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Broader external or collaborative experience** contributing to research proposals, reusable software, publications or multi-partner experimental programmes.

## Additional Duties

- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- May supervise students, placements or discrete tasks undertaken by less experienced colleagues
- May hold delegated responsibility for defined study budgets or resources.

# Principal Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Principal Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** I  
**Reports to:** Head of Experimental Design or equivalent senior leader  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Principal Experimental Design Engineer is a recognised subject-matter expert who leads and is accountable for one significant computational, experimental or integrated capability, exercising authoritative judgement on strategically important problems with limited or emerging precedent.

The purpose of this role is to lead a significant experimental-design capability within a broader research and engineering portfolio. The role integrates computation, advanced diagnostics and practical experimentation across sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, high-performance scientific computing and test delivery.

By establishing sustainable capability and authoritative evidence, the role improves major programme decisions, prioritises scarce resources effectively and accelerates translation of experimental design methods into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **authoritative technical knowledge** to divisional and programme needs, providing specialist advice and trusted capability direction for sponsors, senior technical authorities and strategic partners.
- Establish and shape approaches for **significant, complex and strategically important** problems with limited or emerging precedent, converting uncertain needs into authoritative methods and investment choices.
- Lead and be accountable for a **capability** by approving significant decisions and prioritising substantial people, budgets, facilities, computing and experimental resources against a multiyear roadmap.
- Convert programme evidence needs and organisational priorities into an integrated computational-experimental capability strategy with measurable outcomes for major projects and sponsors.
- Review proposed methods, architectures, diagnostics and validation approaches against capability standards, producing authorised solutions or corrective direction across major projects.
- Translate strategic capability gaps into sponsored development of algorithms, software, diagnostics and test methods, producing reusable organisational assets for multiple programmes.
- Convert cross-project dependencies into coordinated responsibilities and interfaces among senior specialists and partner organisations, protecting major capability and programme outcomes.
- Review complex methods, plans and evidence outputs against delegated capability requirements, providing final technical sign-off or escalation for programme sponsors and senior authorities.
- Convert roadmap priorities, resource constraints and infrastructure needs into funded and sequenced capability plans that improve delivery, resilience and long-term sustainability.
- Translate capability strategy into substantial proposals, publications, software platforms, datasets and external partnerships that strengthen funding, reputation and access to expertise.
- Convert authoritative capability evidence into advice on divisional strategy and investment, influencing programme, infrastructure and resource decisions by senior leadership.
- Translate methods, software and experimental capability into sustained adoption across fusion and adjacent sectors, producing measurable impact for internal programmes and external partners.
- Convert broad functional guidelines into technical policy, standards and operating principles that ensure consistent practice across programmes, facilities and partner activities.
- Provide authoritative advice, external representation and leadership development from capability experience, influencing senior stakeholders while building succession and future technical leaders.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive and broad experience across experimental design, computational research, engineering experimentation or a complementary combination of these.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of original research and technical leadership may be advantageous.
- **Authoritative core knowledge** of applied experimental design and the integration of computational models, physical experiments, measurements, uncertainty and engineering decisions.
- **Sustained and significant experience** leading and being accountable for a capability addressing complex and strategically important problems where precedent is limited or emerging.
- **Authoritative knowledge and sustained experience** in one or more specialist areas, such as advanced scientific software, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement science, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Comprehensive breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to exercise authoritative judgement and lead senior specialists.
- **Authoritative knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing and the strategic routes from efficient scripting to compiled, parallel, distributed or accelerator implementations.
- **Comprehensive knowledge** of model credibility, calibration, traceability, measurement uncertainty and evidence quality for significant programme decisions.
- **Sustained experience** establishing technical standards, assurance approaches and reusable capability across major projects.
- **Integrated and strategic experience** prioritising substantial capability resources and balancing technical ambition, feasibility, cost, schedule, risk and information value.
- **Sustained and significant experience** communicating and defending technical direction through major proposals, publications, reviews, advisory activity and external partnerships.
- **Sustained and significant experience** acting as an industry-recognised expert, providing final technical sign-off, authoritative specialist advice and national or international representation within a significant capability.
- **Proven experience** providing technical or matrix leadership across senior contributors and developing future technical leaders.

### Desirable

- **Comprehensive specialist knowledge** in a second advanced area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental authority.
- **Integrated and strategic breadth** spanning both advanced scientific computing and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Strategic external experience** supporting divisional roadmaps, major investment decisions or national and international collaborations.
- Chartered status or equivalent professional standing where relevant.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management or matrix-management responsibility.
- May hold budgetary authority and responsibility for significant capability resources, facilities or infrastructure.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

# Head of Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Head of Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** J  
**Reports to:** Divisional Director, Programme Director or equivalent  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Head of Experimental Design is a nationally and internationally recognised expert who directs and governs a major multiyear organisational function, provides technical strategic leadership and independently leads delivery across a portfolio of computational and experimental capability.

The purpose of this role is to create, sustain and direct a major organisational function in experimental design. The role integrates computational engineering, advanced diagnostics and physical experimentation across programmes, and governs priorities, resources, standards and external partnerships.

By establishing new research directions and converting them into sustained organisational capability, the role strengthens scientific leadership, accelerates experimental design innovation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, and creates significant economic impact.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **strategic and authoritative knowledge** to organisational objectives, providing strategic specialist advice and directing sustained outcomes for senior leadership, programme directors and external partners.
- Create and direct approaches for **major, highly ambiguous and organisation-shaping** challenges with little precedent, converting competing strategic needs into coherent functional direction.
- Direct and govern a **function** by setting priorities and allocating or reallocating substantial people, budgets, facilities, computing and experimental resources against long-term organisational strategy.
- Convert organisational fusion priorities into functional strategy, standards and assurance frameworks that align multiple programmes, capabilities and senior stakeholders.
- Translate emerging scientific and engineering needs into new research directions, producing sustained programmes, facilities, software, methods and partnerships for the organisation.
- Convert organisational assurance requirements into governed technical approaches, validation strategies and evidence standards that provide consistent decision quality across programmes.
- Translate functional priorities into directed programmes, multidisciplinary teams and Principal-level responsibilities that deliver coherent organisational outcomes.
- Review major engineering and scientific programmes against functional evidence needs, producing authoritative challenge, approvals or corrective direction for programme directors and senior leadership.
- Convert external funding opportunities and strategic needs into major proposals, partnerships and collaborations that expand organisational capability, influence and income.
- Use portfolio performance, risk and resource evidence to allocate and reallocate functional resources, producing balanced delivery and sustainable long-term capability.
- Translate functional strategy into representation and agreements with industry, academia, funders and national or international bodies that strengthen partnerships and external standing.
- Convert integrated computational-experimental capability into sustained industrial and cross-sector adoption, producing wider scientific, engineering and economic impact.
- Review major functional methods, policies and outputs, providing final technical sign-off and governed operating standards for programmes and multidisciplinary teams.
- Negotiate organisational and external priorities using portfolio evidence, securing commitment to functional strategy while producing a sustainable leadership and succession pipeline.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of original scientific and engineering achievement would normally be expected.
- **Strategic and authoritative core knowledge** of experimental design and the integration of computational models, physical experiments, measurement systems, uncertainty and organisational decision-making.
- **Extensive and sustained experience** directing and governing a major multiyear organisational function addressing highly ambiguous and organisation-shaping challenges.
- **Strategic and authoritative knowledge with extensive experience** in one or more specialist areas, such as advanced scientific computing, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement science, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Organisation-wide breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to direct Principal-level specialists and govern integrated capability.
- **Strategic knowledge** of performance-aware computing and the investment routes required to deliver scalable, sustainable and accessible scientific software.
- **Organisation-wide knowledge** of evidence quality, model credibility, measurement assurance, uncertainty and risk in major engineering decisions.
- **Extensive and sustained experience** establishing organisational standards, assurance frameworks, technical governance and reusable capability across programmes.
- **Cross-programme and organisational experience** allocating substantial resources and balancing competing priorities, partnerships, facilities and long-term capability needs.
- **Extensive and sustained experience** influencing senior leadership, major funders and external partners through strategy, proposals, publications and advisory activity.
- **Extensive and sustained experience** acting as final technical authority for a major function, formulating policy, governing standards and directing large multidisciplinary teams or networks.
- **Organisation-wide experience** representing a major function and providing strategic specialist advice to senior leadership, funders and national or international partners.
- **Proven experience** leading Principal-level contributors, major collaborations and succession across a substantial function.

### Desirable

- **Organisation-wide specialist knowledge** in a second advanced area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental authority.
- **Cross-programme breadth** spanning both advanced scientific computing and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Organisational and external experience** shaping national or international research agendas, standards or major partnerships.
- Chartered status, Fellowship of an appropriate professional body or equivalent standing.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management and/or matrix-management responsibility for a substantial team, portfolio or network.
- May hold authority over major budgets, facilities, resources and organisational technical approvals.
- Undertake national and international travel as required.

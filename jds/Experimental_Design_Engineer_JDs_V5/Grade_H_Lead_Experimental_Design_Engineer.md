# Lead Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** H  
**Reports to:** Principal Experimental Design Engineer or Head of Experimental Design  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Lead Experimental Design Engineer is a subject-matter expert who is accountable for the technical outcomes of major computational, experimental or integrated projects, setting technical strategic direction for highly complex and multidisciplinary problems with limited precedent.

The purpose of this role is to lead projects that combine advanced computation, physical experimentation and measurement science to resolve complex fusion engineering challenges. The role sets technical direction across substantial bodies of work spanning computational experimental design, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, data acquisition and performance-aware computing.

By creating coherent computational-experimental evidence, the role protects major programme outcomes, improves use of specialist facilities and accelerates deployment of new experimental design methods into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **deep technical knowledge** to major project objectives, providing expert advice and technical strategic leadership that produces coherent direction for programme sponsors, capability leads and partner organisations.
- Integrate and innovate methods for **highly complex and multidisciplinary** problems with limited precedent, converting competing technical, operational and evidential constraints into an agreed project approach.
- **Be accountable for a project** by setting direction, approving key outputs and coordinating substantial people, budgets, facilities, computing and experimental resources to deliver agreed programme outcomes.
- Convert programme evidence needs into integrated approaches across simulation, measurement modelling, practical test design, validation and uncertainty that align related work packages and stakeholders.
- Translate identified capability gaps into new computational and experimental methods, producing deployable tools or processes for project teams and organisational users.
- Convert assurance requirements into project standards for software, instrumentation, calibration and data quality that reduce duplication and conflicting practice across work packages.
- Convert complex experimental objectives into coordinated campaigns involving multiple diagnostics, facilities and constraints, producing credible evidence for major engineering decisions.
- Translate cross-system requirements into coordinated architectures and interfaces across software, instrumentation, datasets and analysis workflows that provide interoperable capability for programme users.
- Review significant methods, plans, results and evidence packages against project requirements, producing delegated approvals or corrective actions for work-package owners and sponsors.
- Convert highly complex simulation–experiment discrepancies into integrated investigations, producing resolved causes, directed corrective actions and residual-risk advice for programme stakeholders.
- Use project risk, schedule and evidence needs to reprioritise substantial resources, producing revised delivery plans that protect major outcomes and escalate consequences beyond delegated authority.
- Convert project strategy and results into major proposals, publications, software capabilities, datasets and collaborations that strengthen organisational capability and reputation.
- Convert multidisciplinary interfaces into assigned responsibilities, reviewed outputs and specialist development, ensuring internal and external contributors deliver consistently across the project.
- Reconcile conflicting technical positions using project evidence and stakeholder needs, producing agreed methods, priorities and decisions across facilities, programmes and partner organisations.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive relevant experience or a doctorate supported by a strong record of technical, research and project leadership.
- **Deep core knowledge** of applied experimental design and how computational models, physical experiments, measurements, uncertainty and decisions are integrated across major projects.
- **Extensive experience** being accountable for highly complex and multidisciplinary projects where precedent is limited.
- **Deep knowledge and extensive experience** in one or more specialist areas, such as advanced scientific software, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement systems, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Extensive breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to set project direction and integrate expert contributions.
- **Deep knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing and the routes from scripting workflows to compiled, vector, parallel, distributed or accelerator implementations.
- **Deep knowledge** of calibration, traceability, model credibility, measurement uncertainty and evidence quality for major engineering decisions.
- **Extensive experience** establishing technical standards, assurance approaches and reusable capability across multiple work packages.
- **Extensive experience** coordinating substantial project resources and making delegated decisions across people, facilities, computing and experimental activity.
- **Multidisciplinary experience** resolving interfaces between software, hardware, facilities, models, measurements and operational constraints.
- **Extensive experience** communicating and defending technical strategy through major reports, publications, proposals, reviews and external forums.
- **Extensive experience** acting as a subject-matter expert, providing technical strategic advice, delegated approval and specialist development across multidisciplinary projects.
- **Proven experience** providing technical or matrix leadership across multidisciplinary teams and developing emerging technical leaders.

### Desirable

- **Extensive specialist knowledge** in a second advanced area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental route.
- **Extensive multidisciplinary experience** spanning both advanced scientific computing and advanced diagnostics or measurement systems.
- **Multidisciplinary external experience** leading national or international collaborations, major facilities activity or externally funded programmes.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management and/or matrix-management responsibility for a multidisciplinary team.
- May hold delegated authority for substantial project budgets, resources, facilities and technical approvals.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

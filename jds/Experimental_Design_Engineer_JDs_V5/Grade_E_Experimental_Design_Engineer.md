# Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** E  
**Reports to:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer or Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

An Experimental Design Engineer is a developing subject-matter specialist who contributes to defined computational and experimental tasks by applying established methods to moderately complex engineering problems with guidance from senior colleagues.

The purpose of this role is to develop, verify and apply computational and practical experimental methods that improve the quality, efficiency and credibility of fusion engineering validation. The role supports the design, simulation, delivery and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. It combines applied experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, practical instrumentation, data acquisition, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware scientific computing.

By connecting computational predictions with real measurements, the role contributes reliable technical evidence for engineering decisions, reduces unnecessary testing, makes efficient use of assigned computing and experimental resources, and supports translation of experimental design methods into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Apply **demonstrable technical knowledge** to defined computational or experimental requirements, providing specialist advice and producing reliable analysis or measurement outputs for the immediate team, laboratory colleagues and internal users.
- Apply established methods to **moderately complex** engineering problems with clear precedent, converting defined inputs into technically justified results and escalating unresolved departures so senior colleagues can protect task quality and delivery.
- Contribute to an assigned **task** by using allocated computing, equipment and test time efficiently, delivering agreed outputs to schedule for project engineers and the immediate technical team.
- Convert clearly defined engineering questions and acceptance needs into experimental-design, measurement-modelling, validation and uncertainty calculations that provide traceable evidence for project decisions.
- Translate mathematical descriptions, algorithms and software requirements into tested, maintainable and suitably performant implementations that internal software users can apply reliably.
- Convert validated methods into accessible Python interfaces, examples and reproducible workflows that reduce specialist support needs for engineers and researchers.
- Use profiling and benchmarking evidence to identify avoidable performance limitations and implement agreed low-risk changes that reduce runtime, computing demand or repeated effort for the team.
- Convert test plans and instrumentation requirements into installed sensors, configured acquisition channels and completed test documentation that support safe and repeatable experiments for laboratory teams.
- Apply defined calibration and verification procedures to sensors, cameras and measurement chains, producing traceable calibration records and quantified limitations for experimental teams.
- Process raw experimental data using established methods, producing checked datasets and visualisations while flagging anomalies so senior colleagues can make timely corrective decisions.
- Convert software and experimental requirements into automated tests, verification cases and structured records that improve reproducibility, reliability and auditability for internal users and reviewers.
- Compare simulation and measurement inputs using defined metrics, uncertainty estimates and acceptance criteria, producing a clear statement of agreement or discrepancy for project stakeholders.
- Proactively identify opportunities from assigned workflows and user feedback, recommending improvements that increase quality, efficiency or repeatability for the immediate team.
- Convert completed technical work into reports, code documentation, experimental records and presentations that allow internal and external technical audiences to review, understand and reuse the methods and results.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience gained within a structured research or engineering environment, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- **Demonstrable core knowledge** of applied experimental design, including how engineering questions, computational models, physical measurements, uncertainty and acceptance evidence are connected.
- **Practical experience** contributing to defined tasks involving moderately complex computational, experimental or integrated problems, using established methods with some guidance.
- **Demonstrable knowledge** of relevant engineering mathematics, numerical methods and physical-modelling principles used to interpret simulation and experimental results.
- **Demonstrable knowledge and practical experience** in one or more foundational skills, such as scientific Python, numerical analysis, sensors, instrumentation, data acquisition, calibration, data processing or measurement modelling.
- **Demonstrable knowledge and practical experience** in one or more advanced experimental-design skills, such as compiled or systems programming, profiling and optimisation, advanced sensors, imaging diagnostics, signal processing, uncertainty analysis or design of experiments.
- **Developing breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, with sufficient understanding to collaborate with specialists and recognise when senior guidance is required.
- **Developing knowledge** of how model assumptions, numerical error, sensor behaviour, test conditions, data quality and uncertainty affect engineering conclusions.
- **Practical experience** creating reproducible computational or experimental workflows using appropriate testing, documentation, version control or structured laboratory records.
- **Relevant experience** using assigned computing, equipment or experimental resources efficiently to contribute reliable outputs to a technical team.
- **Relevant experience** comparing simulations and measurements using defined metrics, uncertainty estimates or acceptance criteria.
- **Relevant experience** communicating technical work through reports, presentations, software documentation, experimental records or collaborative project outputs.
- **Relevant experience** providing technical advice within a team and proactively identifying improvements to established computational or experimental methods.
- **Developing capability** to plan assigned work, resolve routine technical problems, recommend improvements and escalate departures from established methods.

### Desirable

- **Developing specialist knowledge** of compiled-language software, parallel computing, vectorisation, accelerator programming or other performance-aware implementation approaches.
- **Developing breadth** through knowledge of advanced diagnostics such as digital image correlation (DIC), infrared thermography, high-speed imaging, photogrammetry or related data-rich measurement techniques.
- **Relevant external or collaborative experience** contributing to publications, conference material, reusable software, experimental datasets or multi-partner research outputs.

## Additional Duties

- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- Complete role-specific training required to work safely and effectively in laboratories and specialist facilities.
- Provide informal technical support to colleagues, students or new team members.

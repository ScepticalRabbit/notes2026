# Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** E  
**Reports to:** Senior or Lead Experimental Design Engineer
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

An Experimental Design Engineer implements, verifies and improves clearly defined computational and experimental methods under the guidance of senior colleagues.

The purpose of this role is to develop, verify and apply computational and practical experimental methods that improve the quality, efficiency and credibility of fusion engineering validation. The role supports the design, simulation, delivery and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. It combines computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, practical instrumentation, data acquisition, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware programming. Working within clearly defined technical objectives and established methods, the role helps engineers obtain stronger evidence from finite experimental resources.

By connecting computational predictions with real measurements, the role improves confidence in engineering decisions, reduces unnecessary testing, makes better use of specialist facilities and supports the translation of methods and software into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors. 

## Accountabilities

- Develop clearly defined computational tools for the design, simulation and analysis of validation experiments, working from technical specifications and within established scientific and software architectures.
- Apply established methods selected by senior colleagues for experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, data analysis, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, escalating where existing methods are inadequate.
- Translate mathematical descriptions, algorithms and engineering requirements into tested, clear, maintainable and performant computational implementations.
- Develop and maintain accessible Python interfaces to validated computational methods and, where required, performant implementations.
- Apply profiling and benchmarking tools to identify avoidable performance limitations and implement agreed improvements.
- Contribute to the preparation and delivery of laboratory experiments, including selection and installation of suitable sensors, basic instrumentation, data-acquisition configuration and test documentation.
- Support calibration, characterisation and verification of sensors, cameras, data-acquisition systems and associated measurement chains.
- Process, visualise and assess experimental data, identifying obvious anomalies, data-quality problems and departures from expected behaviour.
- Implement automated tests, verification cases, documentation and reproducible workflows for computational and experimental methods.
- Contribute to comparison of simulated and measured quantities using defined validation and uncertainty approaches.
- Record and communicate technical work clearly through internal reports, code documentation, presentations and structured experimental records.
- Work safely in laboratory and computing environments, following relevant procedures and escalating technical, quality or safety concerns.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

The role holder must demonstrate the core experimental-design competence described below. They must also demonstrate developing capability in at least one specialist route: computational or experimental. Equal depth across both specialisms is not required, but sufficient breadth must be maintained to contribute to integrated computational and experimental work and to develop capability in the weaker area.

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience gained within a structured research or engineering environment, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- Demonstrable practical experience applying quantitative methods to engineering or scientific problems involving experimental, simulation or combined evidence.
- Willingness to undertake role-specific laboratory, instrumentation, software and safety training.

### Core Experimental Design Knowledge and Experience

- Developing ability to translate a defined engineering question into measurable quantities, experimental objectives and evidence requirements.
- Sound understanding of relevant engineering mathematics, numerical methods and physical-modelling principles.
- Awareness of how computational models, sensors, test conditions, data quality and uncertainty interact.
- Ability to compare predictions and measurements using defined metrics, uncertainty estimates and acceptance criteria.
- Developing understanding of how experimental-design choices affect information content, practical feasibility, cost, risk and interpretability.

### Computational Specialism: Knowledge and Experience

- Intermediate knowledge of Python for scientific or engineering computation, including numerical array programming, testing, documentation and reusable modules or packages.
- Demonstrable ability to translate clearly defined mathematical, scientific or engineering requirements into working computational implementations.
- Basic knowledge of compiled languages or systems-programming concepts, with the ability to contribute to established performance-critical code under guidance.
- Familiarity with version control, automated testing, reproducible environments and collaborative software-development practices.
- Awareness of profiling, benchmarking, memory use, numerical efficiency and the relationship between algorithm design and computational performance.

### Experimental Specialism: Knowledge and Experience

- Broad practical understanding of common engineering sensors and measurement systems, such as strain, displacement, temperature, force, pressure, current, voltage or imaging-based measurements.
- Familiarity with basic data-acquisition concepts, including sampling, synchronisation, signal conditioning, sensor range, resolution and noise.
- Ability to contribute to laboratory test preparation, instrumentation setup, calibration checks and structured data collection under guidance.
- Awareness of common sources of experimental error, uncertainty, drift, alignment error, environmental sensitivity and data loss.
- Exposure to advanced diagnostics such as digital image correlation (DIC), infrared thermography, high-speed imaging or related data-rich techniques is advantageous but not essential.

### Research, Communication and Leadership Experience

- Ability to plan and prioritise assigned work and deliver reliable outputs to agreed timescales.
- Clear written and verbal communication, including concise and coherent technical reporting and presentation of results.
- Ability to work effectively with computational scientists, experimental engineers, technicians and domain specialists.
- Ability to contribute to publications, conference material, software documentation or technical reports under guidance.
- Willingness to share knowledge, provide informal technical support and develop across both computational and experimental practice.

## Additional Duties

- Present technical work internally and, where appropriate, at external meetings, workshops or conferences.
- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- Complete role-specific training required to work safely and effectively in laboratories and specialist facilities.
- Provide informal technical support to colleagues, students or new team members.

# Senior Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** G  
**Reports to:** Lead or Principal Experimental Design Engineer 
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Senior Experimental Design Engineer defines and leads technical work packages, owns methods or specialist capability areas, and provides technical or matrix leadership across computational and experimental activity for fusion engineering validation.

The purpose of this role is to define and lead significant integrated work packages that combine computation, instrumentation and experiment to answer difficult fusion engineering questions. The role owns substantial technical components, methods or specialist capability areas spanning computational experimental design, practical test design, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification, data acquisition and performance-aware computing. It converts broad project objectives into deliverable computational and experimental work, coordinates contributors and develops new approaches where established methods do not fully meet the need. 

By improving the quality, efficiency and traceability of validation evidence, the role enables stronger engineering decisions, accelerates development of fusion-relevant materials and components, and supports translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors. 

## Accountabilities

- Define technical work packages from broader project objectives, establishing scope, methods, delivery plans, dependencies and acceptance evidence.
- Select, adapt and approve computational and experimental methods within assigned areas and lead development of new approaches where established methods are insufficient.
- Own the technical quality of major software components, measurement methods, diagnostic workflows or specialist capability areas.
- Define requirements for performance, verification, calibration, interoperability, documentation, maintainability and experimental traceability within assigned work.
- Develop integrated experimental-design approaches linking physical models, sensor behaviour, test conditions, data acquisition and validation metrics.
- Lead design and delivery of experimental campaigns or computational-experimental studies, including instrumentation strategy and data-quality assurance.
- Review and approve sensor selection, measurement layouts, acquisition strategies, uncertainty budgets and analysis workflows within delegated scope.
- Coordinate computational, experimental and domain specialists through technical or matrix leadership, assigning work and reviewing outputs.
- Diagnose complex discrepancies between prediction and measurement and lead structured investigations into model, software, instrumentation or test causes.
- Manage work-package resources, risks, interfaces and technical decisions, escalating issues with wider programme consequences.
- Lead technical publications, proposals, software releases, experimental datasets and external collaborations within assigned areas.
- Mentor less experienced engineers and researchers and contribute to capability development across the discipline.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

The role holder must demonstrate the core experimental-design competence described below. They must also demonstrate strong specialist capability in at least one route: computational or experimental, but sufficient breadth must be maintained to lead integrated computational and experimental work and make sound technical decisions across both.

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive relevant experience, or a doctorate with demonstrable postdoctoral, industrial or technical-leadership experience.
- Demonstrable experience leading integrated experimental-design work packages involving computational, experimental or combined technical activity.

### Core Experimental Design Knowledge and Experience

- Advanced ability to convert broad engineering objectives into experimental questions, measurable outputs, modelling requirements and acceptance evidence.
- Advanced knowledge in at least one area of experimental design, measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification or numerical simulation, with working breadth across the wider discipline.
- Ability to define validation and uncertainty strategies for significant projects and assess interactions between models, measurements and data quality.
- Experience selecting and adapting design-of-experiments, optimisation, sensitivity-analysis and uncertainty-propagation methods.
- Ability to develop integrated computational-experimental approaches for complex problems where established procedures are incomplete.

### Computational Specialism: Knowledge and Experience

- Advanced knowledge of Python for scientific computing, engineering analysis and reusable tool development.
- Strong knowledge of one or more compiled or systems-programming languages used for performance-critical scientific software.
- Demonstrable experience profiling, optimising and scaling computational methods across modern central processing unit (CPU), vector, parallel or accelerator architectures.
- Experience defining software architecture, testing, interfaces and development practices for significant libraries, subsystems or scientific workflows.
- Ability to review computational methods for numerical correctness, robustness, maintainability and performance and to integrate them into reproducible engineering workflows.

### Experimental Specialism: Knowledge and Experience

- Strong practical knowledge of engineering sensors, instrumentation, data-acquisition systems and measurement-chain design.
- Experience defining instrumentation strategies for complex tests, including sensor selection, placement, calibration, synchronisation and environmental constraints.
- Ability to lead practical experimental activity and review test readiness, data quality, traceability and uncertainty.
- Experience diagnosing difficult instrumentation, acquisition or interpretation problems.
- Working knowledge of advanced diagnostics such as digital image correlation (DIC), infrared thermography, high-speed imaging, photogrammetry, acoustic or electromagnetic measurements, or equivalent techniques.

### Research, Communication and Leadership Experience

- Demonstrable experience leading technical outputs, publications, proposals, software tools or experimental datasets.
- Mandatory experience coordinating and reviewing the work of others through technical or matrix leadership.
- Ability to communicate complex technical issues to specialist and non-specialist audiences.
- Recognised internally as a specialist in a defined area, with growing external visibility.
- Experience mentoring engineers, researchers, students or technical staff and building collaborations across projects and organisations.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management responsibility and is expected to provide technical management, task coordination, mentoring and review.
- May hold delegated responsibility for work-package budgets, computing resources, laboratory activity or experimental equipment.
- Represent the organisation at national and international technical meetings, conferences and collaborations.
- Undertake national or international travel where required.

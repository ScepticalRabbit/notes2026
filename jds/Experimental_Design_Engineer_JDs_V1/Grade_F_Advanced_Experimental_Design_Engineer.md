# Advanced Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Advanced Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** F  
**Reports to:** Senior or Lead Experimental Design Engineer   
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

An Advanced Experimental Design Engineer independently selects, implements, verifies and applies established computational and experimental methods, taking responsibility for complete technical components and defined studies.

The purpose of this role is to independently deliver complete computational and experimental components that strengthen fusion engineering validation and make better use of scarce test resources. The role develops and applies computational methods, reusable engineering tools and practical measurement approaches for the design, simulation, delivery and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. It works independently across experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, instrumentation, data acquisition, engineering validation, uncertainty quantification and performance-aware programming, taking responsibility for complete technical components and defined studies within larger programmes.

By improving the information gained from experiments and connecting predictive models with traceable measurements, the role supports risk-aware engineering decisions, reduces unnecessary testing and accelerates translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors. 

## Accountabilities

- Interpret engineering and research objectives to develop computational tools for the design, simulation and analysis of validation experiments within established scientific and software frameworks.
- Select and apply appropriate established methods for experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, data analysis, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, escalating where existing methods are inadequate.
- Translate mathematical descriptions, algorithms and engineering requirements into performant, tested, clear and maintainable computational implementations using Python and compiled languages.
- Independently develop and maintain accessible interfaces to validated computational methods and performance-critical implementations.
- Use profiling, benchmarking and scaling studies to identify performance limitations and implement appropriate improvements.
- Plan and deliver defined experimental activities, including sensor selection, instrumentation layout, data-acquisition configuration, calibration checks and test documentation.
- Select appropriate sensors and acquisition parameters for defined engineering measurements, recognising limits arising from range, bandwidth, resolution, synchronisation, noise and environmental conditions.
- Process and interpret experimental data, diagnose common data-quality issues and recommend corrective actions within the scope of the study.
- Develop automated verification cases, validation workflows, uncertainty analyses and reproducible computational-experimental pipelines.
- Compare simulation predictions and measurements using appropriate metrics, identify significant discrepancies and support investigation of their causes.
- Produce technical reports, software documentation, experimental records and publication-quality outputs.
- Support less experienced colleagues and contribute technical advice within the role holder's area of developing specialism.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

The role holder must demonstrate the core experimental-design competence described below. They must also demonstrate established capability in at least one specialist route: computational or experimental. Equal depth across both specialisms is not required, but they must maintain sufficient working breadth in the other route to independently deliver integrated computational and experimental studies and continue developing the weaker area.

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- A relevant doctorate, or equivalent engineering research experience gained within a structured research, industrial or technical environment.
- Practical experience contributing to the delivery and analysis of engineering experiments, simulations or integrated computational-experimental studies.

### Core Experimental Design Knowledge and Experience

- Ability to translate defined engineering and research questions into measurable quantities, experimental objectives, modelling requirements and evidence needs.
- Strong understanding of relevant engineering mathematics, numerical methods, physical modelling and data interpretation.
- Ability to apply established design-of-experiments, sensitivity-analysis, validation and uncertainty-quantification methods.
- Good understanding of how model assumptions, sensor behaviour, test conditions, data quality and uncertainty affect engineering conclusions.
- Ability to assess whether computational and experimental methods are appropriate for a defined question and to establish suitable acceptance criteria.

### Computational Specialism: Knowledge and Experience

- Strong knowledge of Python for scientific computing, including numerical array programming, reusable package development, testing, documentation and data workflows.
- Ability to independently translate mathematical, scientific and engineering requirements into robust computational implementations.
- Working knowledge of at least one compiled language or systems-programming environment used for performance-critical scientific computing.
- Experience using profiling and benchmarking to improve computational performance, memory efficiency or parallel execution.
- Good understanding of version control, automated testing, continuous integration, reproducible environments and clean interfaces between high-level workflows and lower-level implementations.

### Experimental Specialism: Knowledge and Experience

- Broad practical knowledge of common engineering sensors, instrumentation and data-acquisition systems.
- Experience planning and delivering defined measurement activities, including sensor choice, installation, calibration, channel configuration and quality checks.
- Good understanding of sampling, synchronisation, signal conditioning, dynamic range, noise, filtering, drift and measurement-chain traceability.
- Ability to identify and resolve common instrumentation and data-quality problems without close supervision.
- Familiarity with one or more advanced diagnostics such as digital image correlation (DIC), infrared thermography, high-speed imaging, photogrammetry or a related data-rich technique is advantageous.

### Research, Communication and Leadership Experience

- Experience delivering complete technical components or defined research studies within larger programmes.
- Ability to produce clear reports, documentation, presentations, papers or conference outputs.
- Ability to collaborate effectively with computational, experimental and domain specialists and communicate across disciplinary boundaries.
- Ability to supervise students, placements or discrete tasks undertaken by less experienced colleagues.
- Developing specialist knowledge in at least one route while maintaining the breadth needed to integrate computational and experimental work.

## Additional Duties

- Present technical work at internal and external meetings, workshops and conferences.
- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- May supervise students, placements or discrete tasks undertaken by less experienced colleagues.
- May hold delegated budgetary authority for defined activities or small work packages.

# Advanced Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Advanced Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** F  
**Reports to:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer or Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

An Advanced Experimental Design Engineer independently selects, implements, verifies and applies established computational and experimental methods, taking responsibility for complete technical components and defined studies.

The purpose of this role is to independently deliver complete computational and experimental components that strengthen fusion engineering validation and make better use of scarce test resources. The role develops and applies computational methods, reusable engineering tools and practical measurement approaches for the design, simulation, delivery and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. It works independently across experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, instrumentation, data acquisition, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware scientific computing.

By improving the information gained from experiments and connecting predictive models with traceable measurements, the role supports risk-aware engineering decisions, reduces unnecessary testing and accelerates translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Independently select and apply established computational methods to moderately complex and variable validation problems, adapting approaches where required to deliver reliable outputs for project teams and internal users.
- Interpret defined engineering and research objectives to develop complete experimental-design, measurement-modelling, validation and uncertainty workflows that provide decision-ready evidence for project stakeholders.
- Translate mathematical descriptions, algorithms and engineering requirements into robust, tested and maintainable computational implementations that can be reused by engineers and researchers.
- Develop and maintain accessible interfaces between high-level workflows and performance-critical implementations so that internal users can apply specialist methods efficiently and consistently.
- Use profiling, benchmarking and scaling studies to identify performance limitations and implement improvements that reduce runtime, memory use and rework across project analysis workflows.
- Plan and deliver defined experimental activities, selecting sensors, instrumentation layouts and acquisition settings that produce reliable measurements for engineering studies.
- Configure and verify calibration, synchronisation and measurement-chain traceability so that test engineers and analysts can understand the quality and limitations of acquired data.
- Process and interpret experimental data independently, diagnosing common instrumentation and data-quality problems and implementing corrective actions within the study scope.
- Develop automated verification, validation and uncertainty workflows that improve reproducibility and reduce repeated manual effort for project teams.
- Compare simulations and measurements using appropriate metrics and acceptance criteria, identifying significant discrepancies and supporting structured investigation of their causes.
- Produce complete technical reports, software documentation, experimental records and publication-quality outputs that enable review, reuse and external dissemination.
- Present technical work at internal and external meetings, workshops and conferences, adapting communication to project teams, technical specialists and external collaborators.
- Support less experienced colleagues by providing practical advice, reviewing defined tasks and improving the consistency and quality of team outputs.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- A relevant doctorate, or equivalent engineering research experience gained within a structured research, industrial or technical environment.
- **Strong core knowledge** of applied experimental design, including the relationship between engineering questions, models, measurements, uncertainty, evidence quality and decision needs.
- **Established experience** independently delivering moderately complex and variable computational, experimental or integrated studies within defined objectives.
- **Strong knowledge and established experience** in one or more advanced experimental-design skills, selected from areas such as scientific Python, numerical analysis, compiled or systems programming, profiling and optimisation, sensors and instrumentation, data-acquisition architecture, advanced diagnostics, signal processing, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Growing breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, with sufficient capability to integrate digital and physical evidence without routine supervision.
- **Strong knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing at scripting-language level, including profiling, algorithmic efficiency, memory use and appropriate routes to higher-performance implementation.
- **Established experience** designing reproducible computational or experimental workflows using testing, documentation, version control, calibration records or structured data management.
- **Strong knowledge** of how model assumptions, sensor behaviour, test conditions, data quality and uncertainty affect engineering interpretation.
- **Established experience** comparing simulation and measurement outputs and developing appropriate metrics, uncertainty estimates and acceptance criteria.
- **Broader experience** communicating technical work through reports, publications, presentations, software documentation or experimental records.
- **Established capability** to plan and prioritise work, resolve moderately complex technical problems independently and escalate issues with wider project consequences.

### Desirable

- **Growing knowledge** of a second advanced specialist area complementary to the role holder’s primary computational or experimental route.
- **Broader experience** contributing to collaborative research proposals, externally facing software, advanced diagnostics or multi-institution experimental programmes.
- **Established experience** supervising students, placements or discrete tasks undertaken by less experienced colleagues.

## Additional Duties

- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- May supervise students, placements or discrete tasks undertaken by less experienced colleagues.
- May hold delegated budgetary authority for defined activities or small work packages.

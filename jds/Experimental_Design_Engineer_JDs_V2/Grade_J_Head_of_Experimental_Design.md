# Head of Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Head of Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** J  
**Reports to:** Divisional Director, Programme Director or equivalent  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Head of Experimental Design independently leads a major multiyear organisational function or capability portfolio, sets strategic technical direction and is accountable for substantial organisational, scientific and engineering outcomes.

The purpose of this role is to create and direct a major organisational function in experimental design, integrating computational engineering, advanced diagnostics and physical experimentation to establish new research directions and transform fusion validation. The role leads a substantial portfolio of programmes, capabilities and senior technical contributors. It sets cross-cutting direction across computational experimental design, practical experimentation, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, performance-aware scientific computing and the translation of evidence into engineering decisions.

By establishing new areas of research and turning them into sustained engineering capability, the role strengthens organisational scientific leadership, accelerates innovation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, and creates significant economic impact.

## Accountabilities

- Direct a major multiyear experimental-design function or capability portfolio from organisational objectives, delivering sustained scientific, engineering and economic outcomes for senior leadership and programme sponsors.
- Create and set strategic technical direction across computational and experimental validation where precedent is limited across programmes, establishing coherent priorities for Principal-level leaders and capability teams.
- Establish new research directions and convert them into sustained programmes, methods, facilities, software and partnerships that address future fusion delivery needs.
- Govern major technical approaches, evidence standards and validation strategies across the function, providing senior delegated assurance for organisational programmes.
- Prioritise and allocate substantial people, budgets, facilities, computing resources and experimental activity across the portfolio, balancing competing needs and organisational risk.
- Direct multiple significant programmes and coordinate Principal-level specialists, ensuring that independent projects contribute to coherent organisational capability.
- Scrutinise and challenge major engineering and scientific programmes where experimental evidence is critical, improving the quality of decisions made by programme directors and senior leadership.
- Originate and lead nationally or internationally competitive funding proposals, strategic partnerships and external collaborations that expand organisational capability and influence.
- Shape divisional and organisational strategy, investment and capability planning in experimental design, ensuring that resources are aligned with long-term fusion priorities.
- Represent the organisation as a senior authority with industry, academia, funders and national or international bodies, strengthening partnerships and external standing.
- Translate integrated computational and experimental methods into adoption across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, creating wider scientific and economic impact.
- Present and defend portfolio direction to organisational leadership, major funders and strategic partners, enabling decisions on priorities, risk and investment.
- Build a sustainable leadership pipeline, professional community and succession plan across the function, maintaining capability beyond individual programmes.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of original scientific and engineering achievement would normally be expected.
- **Strategic and authoritative core knowledge** of experimental design and the integration of computational models, physical experiments, measurement systems, uncertainty and organisational decision-making.
- **Extensive and sustained experience** independently leading major multiyear functions, portfolios or organisational capabilities.
- **Strategic and authoritative knowledge with extensive experience** in one or more specialist areas, selected from advanced scientific computing, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement science, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Organisation-wide breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to direct Principal-level specialists and govern integrated capability.
- **Strategic knowledge** of performance-aware computing and the investment routes needed to deliver scalable, sustainable and accessible scientific software.
- **Extensive and sustained experience** establishing organisational standards, assurance frameworks, technical governance and reusable capability across programmes.
- **Organisation-wide knowledge** of evidence quality, model credibility, measurement assurance, uncertainty and risk in major engineering decisions.
- **Cross-programme and organisational experience** balancing competing priorities, resources, facilities, partnerships and long-term capability needs.
- **Extensive and sustained experience** influencing senior leadership, major funders and external partners through strategy, proposals, publications and advisory activity.
- **Proven experience** leading Principal-level contributors, major collaborations and succession across a substantial function.

### Desirable

- **Organisation-wide knowledge** of a second advanced specialist route complementary to the role holder’s primary authority.
- **Cross-programme and organisational experience** shaping national or international research agendas, standards or major partnerships.
- Chartered status, Fellowship of an appropriate professional body or equivalent standing.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management and/or matrix-management responsibility for a substantial team, portfolio or network.
- May hold authority over major budgets, facilities, resources and organisational technical approvals.
- Undertake national and international travel as required.

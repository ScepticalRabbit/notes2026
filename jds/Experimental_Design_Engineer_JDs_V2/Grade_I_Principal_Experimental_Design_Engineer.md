# Principal Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Principal Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** I  
**Reports to:** Head of Experimental Design or equivalent senior leader  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Principal Experimental Design Engineer leads one significant capability or project area, providing authoritative technical leadership and accountability for integrated computational and experimental outcomes.

The purpose of this role is to lead a significant experimental-design capability or project within a broader research and engineering portfolio, integrating computation, advanced diagnostics and practical experimentation to shape fusion engineering decisions. The role provides senior technical leadership across experimental programmes, computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, high-performance scientific computing and practical test delivery. It develops and delivers technical roadmaps, coordinates major multidisciplinary activity, may hold delegated authority for significant technical decisions and develops new engineering methodology where precedent is limited or emerging.

By creating stronger evidence for difficult engineering decisions, the role improves the effectiveness of major validation programmes, develops sustainable organisational capability and accelerates translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Shape and lead one significant experimental-design capability or strategically important project area from broad programme objectives, delivering coherent technical direction for major sponsors and divisional stakeholders.
- Establish integrated computational and experimental approaches where precedent is limited or emerging, producing authoritative methods and evidence strategies for complex engineering decisions.
- Authorise significant technical methods, architectures, diagnostics and validation approaches within delegated scope, improving assurance for programme sponsors and senior technical authorities.
- Coordinate major multidisciplinary programmes of software, instrumentation, diagnostics, data and engineering interpretation, ensuring that specialist contributions produce coherent outcomes.
- Sponsor development of new methods, algorithms, diagnostics or test approaches where existing capability is inadequate, creating sustainable capability for multiple projects and internal users.
- Provide final technical review and approval within the assigned capability or project area, escalating decisions with broader organisational consequences.
- Lead senior computational, experimental and domain specialists across projects and partner organisations, resolving interfaces that affect major programme outcomes.
- Plan and coordinate people, budgets, facilities, computing resources and experimental activity, advising on priorities and exercising delegated authority to improve use of scarce resources.
- Lead substantial research proposals, publications, software platforms, datasets and external partnerships that strengthen capability, funding and organisational reputation.
- Contribute authoritative advice to divisional strategy and wider organisational capability and investment planning in experimental design and validation.
- Translate methods, software and experimental capability into strategic use across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, creating impact beyond the immediate programme.
- Present and defend capability direction to divisional leaders, programme sponsors, funders and strategic partners, enabling informed decisions on priorities and investment.
- Develop senior technical leaders, succession and professional standards, ensuring the capability remains sustainable beyond individual projects.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive and broad experience across experimental design, computational research, engineering experimentation or a complementary combination of these.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of original research and technical leadership may be advantageous.
- **Authoritative core knowledge** of applied experimental design and the integration of computational models, physical experiments, measurements, uncertainty and engineering decisions.
- **Sustained and significant experience** leading complex, strategically important computational, experimental or integrated programmes where precedent is limited or emerging.
- **Authoritative knowledge and sustained experience** in one or more specialist areas, selected from advanced scientific software, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement science, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Comprehensive breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to exercise authoritative judgement and lead senior specialists across both.
- **Authoritative knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing and the strategic routes from efficient scripting to compiled, parallel, distributed or accelerator implementations.
- **Sustained experience** establishing technical standards, assurance approaches and reusable capability across major projects or programmes.
- **Comprehensive knowledge** of model credibility, calibration, traceability, measurement uncertainty and evidence quality for significant programme decisions.
- **Integrated and strategic experience** resolving multidisciplinary interfaces and balancing technical ambition, feasibility, cost, schedule, risk and information value.
- **Sustained and significant experience** communicating and defending technical direction through major proposals, publications, reviews, advisory activities and external partnerships.
- **Proven experience** providing technical or matrix leadership across senior contributors and developing future technical leaders.

### Desirable

- **Comprehensive knowledge** of a second advanced specialist route complementary to the role holder’s primary authority.
- **Integrated and strategic experience** supporting divisional capability roadmaps, major investment decisions or national and international collaborations.
- Chartered status or equivalent professional standing where relevant.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management or matrix-management responsibility.
- May hold budgetary authority and responsibility for major projects, facilities or resources.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

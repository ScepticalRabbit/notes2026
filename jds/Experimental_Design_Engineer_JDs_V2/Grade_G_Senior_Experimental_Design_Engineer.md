# Senior Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** G  
**Reports to:** Lead Experimental Design Engineer or Principal Experimental Design Engineer  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Senior Experimental Design Engineer defines and leads moderately complex technical work packages, owns methods or specialist capability areas, and provides technical or matrix leadership across computational and experimental activity for fusion engineering validation.

The purpose of this role is to define and lead integrated work packages that combine computation, instrumentation and experiment to answer difficult fusion engineering questions. The role owns substantial technical components, methods or specialist capability areas spanning computational experimental design, practical test design, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, data acquisition and performance-aware computing. It converts broad project objectives into deliverable work, coordinates contributors and develops new approaches where established methods do not fully meet the need.

By improving the quality, efficiency and traceability of validation evidence, the role enables stronger engineering decisions, accelerates development of fusion-relevant materials and components, and supports translation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Define moderately complex work packages from broad project objectives, establishing scope, methods, dependencies and acceptance evidence that enable work-package owners and multidisciplinary teams to deliver agreed outcomes.
- Lead selection, adaptation and development of computational and experimental methods where precedent is partial, producing fit-for-purpose approaches for project and capability stakeholders.
- Own the technical quality of significant software components, measurement methods, diagnostic workflows or specialist capability areas so that internal users receive reliable and reusable tools.
- Define requirements for performance, verification, calibration, interoperability and traceability, reducing technical risk and inconsistency across related project outputs.
- Develop integrated experimental-design approaches linking physical models, sensors, test conditions, data acquisition and validation metrics to improve the information gained from experiments.
- Lead the design and delivery of moderately complex experimental campaigns or computational-experimental studies, coordinating contributors to produce safe, timely and decision-ready evidence.
- Review and approve sensor selections, measurement layouts, acquisition strategies, uncertainty budgets and analysis workflows within delegated scope, improving test readiness and data quality.
- Coordinate computational, experimental and domain specialists through technical or matrix leadership, assigning work and reviewing outputs to resolve cross-disciplinary interfaces.
- Diagnose complex discrepancies between prediction and measurement, leading structured investigations that identify model, software, instrumentation or test causes for project stakeholders.
- Manage work-package resources, risks and technical decisions, escalating issues with wider programme consequences and improving predictability of delivery.
- Lead technical publications, proposals, software releases, experimental datasets and external collaborations that extend the capability and visibility of the team.
- Present and defend technical recommendations to multidisciplinary project teams, senior specialists and external collaborators, enabling informed agreement on methods and next steps.
- Mentor less experienced engineers and researchers, improving technical consistency, confidence and succession across the discipline.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive relevant experience, or a doctorate with demonstrable postdoctoral, industrial or technical-leadership experience.
- **Advanced core knowledge** of applied experimental design, including how engineering decisions, models, measurements, uncertainty and acceptance evidence are integrated.
- **Substantial experience** defining and leading moderately complex computational, experimental or integrated work packages within broad project objectives.
- **Advanced knowledge and substantial experience** in one or more specialist areas, selected from scientific software, compiled or systems programming, performance optimisation, numerical methods, sensors and instrumentation, data-acquisition systems, advanced diagnostics, measurement modelling, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Broad knowledge** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to lead integrated work and review outputs outside the role holder’s deepest specialism.
- **Advanced knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing, including profiling, algorithm selection, memory behaviour and appropriate use of vector, parallel or accelerator approaches.
- **Substantial experience** establishing reproducible and auditable computational or experimental workflows for multiple users or project teams.
- **Advanced knowledge** of how model form, numerical error, sensor behaviour, calibration, test conditions and uncertainty interact.
- **Wide experience** evaluating alternative methods and making technically justified recommendations where established approaches do not fully fit.
- **Substantial experience** communicating and defending technical outputs through reports, publications, presentations, software or experimental evidence packages.
- **Proven experience** providing technical or matrix leadership, coordinating contributors and reviewing the work of others.

### Desirable

- **Broad knowledge** of a second advanced specialism complementary to the role holder’s primary route.
- **Wide experience** contributing to national or international collaborations, externally funded proposals or shared technical capabilities.
- **Substantial experience** mentoring staff, supervising research activity or supporting recruitment and capability development.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management responsibility and is expected to provide technical management, task coordination, mentoring and review.
- May hold delegated responsibility for work-package budgets, computing resources, laboratory activity or experimental equipment.
- Undertake national or international travel where required.

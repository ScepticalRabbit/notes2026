# Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** E  
**Reports to:** Senior Experimental Design Engineer or Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

An Experimental Design Engineer implements, verifies and improves clearly defined computational and experimental methods under the guidance of senior colleagues.

The purpose of this role is to develop, verify and apply computational and practical experimental methods that improve the quality, efficiency and credibility of fusion engineering validation. The role supports the design, simulation, delivery and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. It combines applied experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, practical instrumentation, data acquisition, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware scientific computing. Working within clearly defined technical objectives and established methods, the role helps engineers obtain stronger evidence from finite experimental resources.

By connecting computational predictions with real measurements, the role improves confidence in engineering decisions, reduces unnecessary testing, makes better use of specialist facilities and supports the translation of methods and software into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Apply established computational methods to moderately complex validation problems, with guidance from senior colleagues, to produce reliable analysis outputs for project engineers and experimental teams.
- Implement clearly defined experimental-design, measurement-modelling, validation and uncertainty methods so that simulations and tests provide traceable evidence for engineering decisions.
- Translate mathematical descriptions, algorithms and engineering requirements into tested, maintainable and suitably performant computational implementations for use by the immediate team and internal software users.
- Develop and maintain accessible Python interfaces and reproducible workflows that allow engineers and researchers to apply validated methods consistently without unnecessary specialist support.
- Apply established profiling and benchmarking methods to moderately complex analysis workflows, implementing agreed improvements that reduce analysis time and computing demand for the team and internal users.
- Contribute to the preparation and delivery of laboratory experiments by installing sensors, configuring data-acquisition systems and producing test documentation that supports safe, repeatable and efficient testing.
- Support calibration, characterisation and verification of sensors, cameras and measurement chains so that experimental teams receive traceable measurements with understood limitations.
- Process, visualise and assess experimental data using established methods, identifying anomalies and data-quality problems so that senior colleagues can make timely corrective decisions.
- Implement automated tests, verification cases and structured records that improve the reproducibility, reliability and auditability of computational and experimental methods.
- Compare simulated and measured quantities using defined metrics, uncertainty estimates and acceptance criteria to provide clear evidence of agreement or discrepancy for project stakeholders.
- Record and communicate methods, assumptions and results through concise reports, code documentation and structured experimental records so that technical outputs can be reviewed, understood and reused by colleagues.
- Present technical work internally and, where appropriate, at external meetings, workshops or conferences, communicating results clearly to technical stakeholders and supporting the wider use and visibility of the team’s methods.
- Work safely within established laboratory and computing procedures, escalating technical, quality and safety concerns so that risks to people, equipment, data and project delivery are controlled.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience gained within a structured research or engineering environment, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- **Demonstrable core knowledge** of applied experimental design, including how engineering questions, computational models, measurements, uncertainty and acceptance evidence are connected.
- **Demonstrable knowledge** of relevant engineering mathematics, numerical methods and physical-modelling principles used to interpret simulation and experimental results.
- **Practical experience** applying quantitative methods to moderately complex engineering or scientific tasks involving experimental data, simulation results or combined evidence.
- **Demonstrable knowledge and practical experience** in one or more foundational computational or experimental skills, such as scientific Python, numerical analysis, sensors, instrumentation, data acquisition, calibration, data processing or measurement modelling.
- **Demonstrable knowledge and practical experience** in one or more advanced experimental-design skills, such as compiled or systems programming, profiling and optimisation, advanced sensors, imaging diagnostics, signal processing, uncertainty analysis or design of experiments.
- **Developing breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, with sufficient understanding to work effectively with specialists and recognise when senior guidance is required.
- **Developing knowledge** of how sensor behaviour, test conditions, model assumptions, data quality and uncertainty can affect engineering conclusions.
- **Practical experience** creating reproducible computational or experimental workflows using appropriate testing, documentation, version control or structured laboratory records.
- **Relevant experience** comparing simulations and measurements using defined metrics, uncertainty estimates or acceptance criteria.
- **Relevant experience** communicating technical work through reports, presentations, software documentation, experimental records or collaborative project outputs.
- **Developing capability** to plan and prioritise assigned work, resolve routine technical problems and escalate moderately complex issues where established methods are inadequate.

### Desirable

- **Developing knowledge** of compiled-language software, parallel computing, vectorisation, accelerator programming or other performance-aware implementation approaches.
- **Developing knowledge** of advanced diagnostics such as digital image correlation (DIC), infrared thermography, high-speed imaging, photogrammetry or related data-rich measurement techniques.
- **Relevant experience** contributing to publications, conference material, reusable software, experimental datasets or collaborative research outputs.

## Additional Duties

- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required.
- Complete role-specific training required to work safely and effectively in laboratories and specialist facilities.
- Provide informal technical support to colleagues, students or new team members.

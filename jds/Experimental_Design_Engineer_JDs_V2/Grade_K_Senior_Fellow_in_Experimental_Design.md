# Senior Fellow in Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Senior Fellow in Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** K  
**Reports to:** Director or higher  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Senior Fellow in Experimental Design provides internationally recognised technical leadership, creates new fields and shapes internal and external research agendas in a specialist subfield.

The purpose of this role is to provide internationally recognised scientific and engineering leadership in experimental design, establishing new fields and shaping the organisation’s long-term technical direction, external standing and impact. The role operates as an organisation-level authority across integrated computational and experimental validation. It creates new methods, research directions and strategic partnerships spanning experimental design, advanced diagnostics, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, performance-aware computing and evidence-led engineering.

By defining the state of the art and translating it into sustainable organisational and industrial capability, the role advances fusion engineering, strengthens national scientific leadership and accelerates innovation into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Originate and lead field-defining research directions in a specialist area of integrated computational and experimental design, creating new scientific and engineering capability for the organisation and wider community.
- Act as an organisation-level authority for unprecedented and strategically critical validation challenges, providing trusted technical judgement to executive leaders and major programmes.
- Shape long-term internal and external research agendas in the specialist field, influencing organisational priorities and national or international technical bodies.
- Define new methods, diagnostics, software architectures, validation frameworks or experimental paradigms where little or no precedent exists, enabling new classes of fusion engineering evidence.
- Sponsor and guide major programmes, capabilities and senior technical leaders across divisions, institutions or sectors, aligning work around field-shaping objectives.
- Provide authoritative scrutiny and challenge to major organisational programmes and evidence strategies, improving decisions with significant scientific, engineering or reputational consequences.
- Influence allocation and prioritisation of substantial organisational resources and strategic investments, ensuring support for critical future capability.
- Originate and lead major national and international partnerships, funding initiatives, advisory activities and research networks that expand the field and organisational influence.
- Represent the organisation at the highest technical levels with industry, academia, funders and national or international bodies, shaping standards, priorities and collaborative agendas.
- Translate frontier research into sustained adoption across fusion and adjacent high-value engineering sectors, creating long-term scientific and economic impact.
- Present and defend field direction to executive leadership, senior technical authorities and international peers, enabling strategic choices on research, risk and investment.
- Develop Principal-level staff, future Fellows and emerging international leaders, strengthening succession within the organisation and wider discipline.
- Protect and enhance the organisation’s long-term scientific reputation, capability and strategic influence by maintaining international leadership in the specialist field.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- A doctorate or equivalent record of sustained original achievement would normally be expected.
- **Internationally recognised authority** in experimental design or a relevant computational, experimental, measurement or validation specialist subfield.
- **Distinguished and sustained experience** creating original methods, programmes, software, diagnostics, facilities or engineering outcomes that have materially influenced the field.
- **Field-shaping knowledge and distinguished experience** in one or more specialist areas, selected from advanced scientific computing, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement science, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **National and international breadth** sufficient to connect the specialist field with complementary computational and experimental disciplines and shape integrated agendas.
- **Internationally recognised knowledge** of performance-aware computing or its strategic relevance to scientific and engineering capability.
- **Distinguished and sustained experience** establishing new frameworks, standards, principles or capability adopted beyond a single programme or organisation.
- **Field-shaping knowledge** of evidence, uncertainty, model credibility, measurement assurance and risk in unprecedented engineering decisions.
- **National and international experience** resolving strategically critical multidisciplinary challenges with little or no precedent.
- **Distinguished and sustained experience** influencing senior leaders, external institutions and international peers through major programmes, publications, advisory roles and partnerships.
- **Proven experience** developing Principal-level staff, Fellows and future international leaders through technical authority and matrix influence.

### Desirable

- Fellowship of an appropriate professional or learned body, chartered status or equivalent international standing where relevant.
- **National and international experience** shaping standards, policy, research agendas or major collaborative programmes.
- **Field-shaping knowledge** extending into a complementary specialist route beyond the role holder’s primary authority.

## Additional Duties

- May lead a substantial function, portfolio or network of technical teams, but line management is not a requirement.
- May hold authority over major budgets, resources, organisational approvals or strategic appointments.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

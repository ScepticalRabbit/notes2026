# Lead Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Lead Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** H  
**Reports to:** Principal Experimental Design Engineer or Head of Experimental Design  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** May be applicable  

## Overall Purpose

A Lead Experimental Design Engineer leads highly complex and multidisciplinary projects or defined capability areas, sets integrated technical direction and is accountable for coordinated delivery through technical or matrix leadership.

The purpose of this role is to lead integrated projects that combine advanced computation, physical experimentation and measurement science to resolve complex fusion engineering challenges. The role sets technical direction across substantial bodies of work spanning computational experimental design, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, sensor and measurement modelling, engineering validation and uncertainty quantification, data acquisition and performance-aware computing. It coordinates multidisciplinary contributors, establishes assurance approaches and develops methods for problems where precedent is limited.

By creating coherent computational-experimental evidence, the role strengthens major engineering decisions, improves the use of specialist facilities and accelerates deployment of new methods and technologies into the fusion industry and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Establish and lead highly complex, multidisciplinary projects or capability areas from broad programme objectives, producing coordinated delivery plans and evidence strategies for major programmes and capability leads.
- Set integrated technical approaches across simulation, measurement modelling, practical test design, validation and uncertainty where precedent is limited, enabling consistent decisions across related projects.
- Direct development of new computational and experimental methods, resolving technical ambiguity and delivering deployable capability for internal teams and partner organisations.
- Establish technical standards for software, instrumentation, data quality, calibration and assurance, reducing duplication and conflicting practice across projects.
- Lead the design and delivery of complex experimental programmes involving multiple diagnostics, facilities and operational constraints, producing credible evidence for major engineering decisions.
- Coordinate architectures and interfaces across software libraries, instrumentation systems, datasets and analysis workflows, ensuring interoperability for capability users and programme teams.
- Act as senior technical reviewer and delegated approver for significant methods, plans, results and evidence packages, improving assurance for programme sponsors and facility owners.
- Provide technical or matrix leadership to multidisciplinary teams, resolving conflicts and coordinating senior contributors so that scarce expertise is deployed effectively.
- Direct investigation of highly complex discrepancies between simulation and experiment, integrating specialist evidence to resolve risks affecting major programme outcomes.
- Influence programme planning, resource allocation, facility use and investment within assigned areas, improving delivery efficiency and long-term capability sustainability.
- Lead major external collaborations, proposals, publications, software capabilities and experimental datasets that strengthen organisational reputation and access to expertise.
- Present and defend technical strategy to senior stakeholders, partner organisations and national or international forums, building consensus around methods, evidence and investment.
- Develop technical leaders and succession pathways, strengthening multidisciplinary capability across teams and programmes.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Essential

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience, in a relevant engineering, physical science, mathematical or computational discipline.
- Extensive relevant experience or a doctorate supported by a strong record of technical, research and project leadership.
- **Deep core knowledge** of applied experimental design and how computational models, physical experiments, measurements, uncertainty and decisions are integrated across major projects.
- **Extensive experience** leading highly complex and multidisciplinary computational, experimental or integrated programmes where precedent is limited.
- **Deep knowledge and extensive experience** in one or more specialist areas, selected from advanced scientific software, systems programming, high-performance computing, numerical methods, instrumentation, advanced diagnostics, measurement systems, validation, uncertainty quantification or design of experiments.
- **Extensive breadth** across complementary computational and experimental methods, sufficient to set technical direction and integrate expert contributions across both.
- **Deep knowledge** of performance-aware scientific computing and the routes from efficient scripting workflows to compiled, vector, parallel, distributed or accelerator implementations.
- **Extensive experience** establishing technical standards, assurance approaches and reusable capability across multiple projects or teams.
- **Deep knowledge** of calibration, traceability, model credibility, measurement uncertainty and evidence quality for significant engineering decisions.
- **Multidisciplinary experience** resolving complex interfaces between software, hardware, facilities, models, measurements and operational constraints.
- **Extensive experience** communicating and defending technical strategy through major reports, publications, proposals, reviews and external forums.
- **Proven experience** providing technical or matrix leadership across multidisciplinary teams and developing less experienced technical leaders.

### Desirable

- **Extensive knowledge** of a second advanced specialist route complementary to the role holder’s primary expertise.
- **Multidisciplinary experience** leading national or international collaborations, major facilities activity or externally funded technical programmes.
- **Extensive experience** contributing to capability roadmaps, succession planning or investment decisions.

## Additional Duties

- May hold line-management and/or matrix-management responsibility for a multidisciplinary team.
- May hold delegated authority for substantial budgets, resources, facilities and technical approvals.
- Undertake national and international travel where required.

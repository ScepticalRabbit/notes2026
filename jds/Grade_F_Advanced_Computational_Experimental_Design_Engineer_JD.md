# Advanced Computational Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Advanced Computational Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** F  
**Reports to:** Senior Computational Experimental Design Engineer (or higher)  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** Normally no  

## Overall Purpose

Develop and apply computational methods and reusable engineering tools for the design, simulation and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. The role works independently across computational experimental design; sensor and measurement modelling; verification, validation and uncertainty quantification (VVUQ); and performance-aware programming to deliver complete technical components and defined research studies.

By improving the information gained from experiments, the role supports risk-aware engineering decisions, more efficient use of experimental resources and the development of sustainable and commercially viable fusion technologies with wider UK economic benefit.

An Advanced Computational Experimental Design Engineer independently selects, implements, verifies and applies established computational methods, taking responsibility for complete technical components and defined research studies within larger programmes.

## Accountabilities

- Interpret defined engineering and research objectives and determine suitable implementation approaches within established scientific and computational frameworks.
- Select and apply appropriate established methods for computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling and VVUQ, escalating where existing methods are inadequate.
- Design, implement and maintain complete algorithms, software components or small libraries using Python and suitable compiled languages.
- Develop accessible Python interfaces to performant computational implementations and ensure that components are usable, documented and maintainable.
- Independently profile and optimise computational components, evaluate alternatives and justify recommendations using reproducible correctness and performance evidence.
- Define and execute verification, regression and reference-comparison activities for assigned components and studies.
- Develop and apply sensor models, uncertainty models, validation metrics and experimental-design methods, documenting assumptions, limitations and applicability.
- Lead defined technical studies, analyse simulation and experimental data, and draw evidence-based conclusions concerning uncertainty, information gain, identifiability and validation evidence.
- Collaborate directly with experimentalists, simulation engineers, measurement specialists and researchers to refine requirements and deliver complete technical outputs.
- Take responsibility for the quality, correctness, performance, documentation and timely delivery of complete components and defined research studies.
- Contribute substantial technical content to publications, conference outputs and grant proposals, and lead first-author outputs where appropriate.
- Review defined technical work and provide informal guidance to less experienced colleagues.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience gained within a structured research environment, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- A relevant doctorate, or equivalent research and engineering experience, may provide an appropriate route of entry.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Ability to independently translate clearly defined mathematical, scientific or engineering requirements into working computational implementations.
- Proficient knowledge of Python for scientific and engineering computation, including package development, testing, documentation and deployment.
- Practical knowledge and experience with C interfaces and interoperability between Python and compiled languages
- Demonstrable experience with at least one compiled systems programming language with explicit control over memory and data representation.
- Practical knowledge and experience with performance-aware programming, including profiling, memory use, data layout, vectorisation, parallelism or accelerator computing.
- Ability to design and verify complete computational components within an established architecture.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Sound knowledge of relevant mathematical and numerical methods, statistics, optimisation, inference and uncertainty propagation.
- Ability to select and apply suitable established methods for bounded technical problems.
- Ability to evaluate assumptions, limitations, numerical behaviour and sources of uncertainty.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- Strong knowledge and practical experience in at least one core capability area, with developing breadth across computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware computational engineering.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- Experience independently delivering research or engineering studies and communicating evidence-based conclusions.
- Ability to produce substantial technical content for publications, conference outputs and proposals.
- Ability to plan and prioritise work with limited supervision and collaborate effectively across disciplines.
- Ability to support and advise less experienced colleagues.

## Additional Duties

- Present technical work at internal and external meetings, workshops and conferences.
- Undertake occasional national or international travel.
- May supervise students, placements or discrete tasks undertaken by less experienced colleagues
- Normally no formal line-management responsibility or delegated budgetary authority.

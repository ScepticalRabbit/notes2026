# Computational Experimental Design Engineer

**Role Title:** Computational Experimental Design Engineer  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** E  
**Reports to:** Senior Computational Experimental Design Engineer (or higher)  
**Skill Type:** Engineering  
**Line Manager:** No  

## Overall Purpose

Develop and apply computational methods and reusable engineering tools for the design, simulation and analysis of materials and component validation experiments in extreme fusion environments. The role combines computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, verification, validation and uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware programming to help engineers obtain the strongest possible evidence from finite experimental resources.

By improving the information gained from experiments, the role supports risk-aware engineering decisions while reducing unnecessary testing, cost, material use and demand on specialist facilities. This contributes to the delivery of sustainable and commercially viable fusion energy and creates UK economic benefit through the transfer of computational methods and design tools into fusion engineering and adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Develop clearly defined computational features and performant reusable components for the design, simulation and analysis of validation experiments, working from technical specifications and within established scientific and computational architectures.
- Implement established mathematical and numerical methods for computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, and verification, validation and uncertainty quantification.
- Translate mathematical descriptions, algorithms and engineering requirements into performant, tested, clear and maintainable computational implementations using Python and, where required, suitable compiled languages.
- Develop and maintain accessible Python interfaces to performant computational components so that methods can be used effectively by fusion engineers, experimentalists and researchers.
- Profile computational modules, algorithms and workflows to identify performance limitations, implement appropriate local improvements, and evaluate alternatives using reproducible performance benchmarks under the guidance of senior colleagues.
- Develop and apply unit tests, numerical verification cases, regression tests and reference comparisons to demonstrate the correctness, robustness and performance of implemented methods.
- Implement and apply sensor models, measurement uncertainty models, validation metrics and uncertainty propagation methods selected or approved by senior colleagues, documenting assumptions, limitations and sources of uncertainty so that outputs can be interpreted appropriately.
- Analyse simulation and experimental data to assess measurement uncertainty, information gain, parameter identifiability and the strength of validation evidence, investigating unexpected numerical, computational or performance behaviour and proposing improvements within the boundaries of the assigned component or work package.
- Work with experimentalists, simulation engineers, measurement specialists and researchers to understand requirements and implement defined computational solutions.
- Take responsibility for the quality, correctness, documentation and timely delivery of discrete technical outputs within larger research and engineering work packages.
- Make implementation decisions within defined technical parameters and escalate decisions concerning scientific methodology, computational architecture, programme risk or significant resource commitments.
- Proactively identify opportunities to improve assigned methods, software components and working practices, and communicate evidence-based recommendations to senior colleagues.
- Produce clear technical documentation, reports, presentations and reproducible research records that enable computational methods and results to be understood, reviewed and used by others.
- Contribute computational methods, verified implementations, analysis, figures, results and written technical content to peer-reviewed publications, conference presentations, technical reports and grant proposals.
- Support the application and transfer of computational experimental-design methods and tools to fusion programmes and relevant adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree, or equivalent demonstrable experience gained within a structured research or engineering environment, in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Intermediate knowledge of Python for scientific or engineering computation, including numerical array programming, testing, documentation and the development of reusable modules or packages.
- Demonstrable ability to translate clearly defined mathematical, scientific or engineering requirements into working computational implementations.
- Basic knowledge of C, including its use in defining interfaces that enable interoperability between software written in different programming languages.
- Basic knowledge of at least one compiled systems programming language with explicit control over memory and data representation, such as C, C++, C3, Odin or Zig.
- Foundational knowledge of performance-aware programming, including efficient numerical array operations, avoidance of unnecessary interpreted loops, awareness of memory allocation, memory layout and data movement, and use of profiling and benchmarking to identify bottlenecks under the guidance of a senior colleague.
- Knowledge of software testing and numerical verification practices, including unit testing, regression testing and comparison against analytical or reference solutions.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Knowledge of relevant numerical and mathematical principles, which may include numerical methods, linear algebra, probability, statistics, optimisation, inference or uncertainty propagation.
- Ability to implement and apply established mathematical or engineering methods selected by a senior colleague.
- Ability to recognise, document and communicate assumptions, limitations and sources of uncertainty.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- Demonstrable knowledge or practical experience in at least one of the following areas, together with the ability and motivation to develop capability across the remaining areas: simulation-driven or Bayesian experimental design; sensor, imaging or measurement-system modelling; measurement uncertainty and uncertainty propagation; mathematical methods for verification, validation and uncertainty quantification; numerical optimisation, statistical inference or information-theoretic methods; image-based measurement, computational imaging or synthetic data generation; or performance-aware numerical or scientific computing.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- Experience contributing to research, engineering development or technical investigation within a structured environment.
- Ability to analyse technical results, maintain reproducible records and draw evidence-based conclusions.
- Ability to communicate mathematical and technical concepts clearly in written and verbal form to colleagues from different engineering and scientific disciplines, including the ability to collaborate effectively with experimentalists, simulation engineers, measurement specialists, researchers and other technical stakeholders.
- Ability to contribute effectively to technical reports, research publications and conference outputs.
- Ability to plan and prioritise assigned activities, work independently within defined parameters and seek guidance or escalate issues appropriately.

## Additional Duties

- Present technical work at internal meetings and, where appropriate, at external technical meetings, workshops or conferences.
- Undertake occasional national or international travel where required to support collaborative research, experimental programmes, conferences or technical meetings.
- Complete role-specific training and development required to work safely and effectively within relevant research, engineering or experimental environments.
- The role has no line-management responsibility and no delegated budgetary authority.
- Provide informal technical support to colleagues, students or new team members within the scope of the job holder's knowledge and assigned work.

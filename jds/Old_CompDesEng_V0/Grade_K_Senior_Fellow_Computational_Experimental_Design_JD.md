# Senior Fellow in Computational Experimental Design

**Role Title:** Senior Fellow in Computational Experimental Design  
**Division / Function:** Fusion Technology Division  
**Grade:** K  
**Reports to:** [Insert job title]  
**Skill Type:** Engineering / Scientific  
**Line Manager:** Not required  

## Overall Purpose

Provide scientific direction and international leadership in computational experimental design, shaping the organisation's long-term strategy across simulation-driven experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, verification, validation and uncertainty quantification, and performance-aware computational engineering. Establish new research directions and ensure that state-of-the-art scientific and computational approaches are used to develop and deliver solutions for fusion engineering.

Through internationally recognised research, strategic partnerships and authoritative scientific direction, position the organisation at the forefront of experimental design and validation for fusion. Create sustained scientific, industrial and UK economic benefit by enabling more informative, resource-efficient validation programmes and transferring methods and computational technologies into adjacent high-value engineering sectors.

## Accountabilities

- Define and lead an internationally recognised programme of research and innovation in computational experimental design and associated validation sciences.
- Provide scientific direction across the organisation in the discipline, shaping long-term strategy, identifying future areas of distinctive capability and advising senior leadership on scientific priorities and investment.
- Establish new research fields, methods and experimental approaches where there is little or no precedent, ensuring that the organisation remains at the state of the art.
- Lead the development of major integrated computational and experimental capabilities spanning simulation-driven design, measurement modelling, VVUQ and performance-aware scientific computing
- Scrutinise, challenge and advise on major organisational scientific and engineering programmes, providing authoritative assessment of their technical direction, credibility and evidence base.
- Influence or establish organisation-wide scientific standards, policies and strategic practice for experimental design, validation, uncertainty quantification and computational research.
- Initiate and lead major national and international collaborations with universities, industry, research organisations and government bodies, positioning the organisation as a leading authority in the field.
- Produce major personal scientific output with international impact, including influential peer-reviewed publications, invited presentations and leadership of internationally significant programmes and proposals.
- Represent the organisation at the highest national and international levels, including strategic advisory groups, expert panels and major collaborative programmes.
- Shape significant organisational and external investment in future scientific capability, identifying opportunities to secure funding, facilities, partnerships and talent.
- Lead the translation and exploitation of computational methods and engineering tools across fusion and adjacent high-value sectors, delivering sustained scientific, industrial and UK economic impact.
- Develop Principal and other senior technical leaders, provide organisation-wide mentorship and act as the senior source of scientific authority and challenge in the discipline.
- Use the enhanced freedom and status of a Senior Fellow to pursue new lines of thought and create transformational research opportunities aligned with the organisation's mission.

## Specific Qualifications / Experience

### Qualifications

- A degree or equivalent demonstrable experience in engineering, physical science, applied mathematics, computer science or another relevant quantitative discipline.
- Deep, broad and comprehensive experience gained through an internationally distinguished research and engineering career. A doctorate or equivalent record of original scientific achievement would normally be expected.
- Chartered status and Fellowship of an appropriate professional or learned body, or equivalent international standing, may be expected where relevant.

### Computational & Programming Knowledge & Experience

- Internationally recognised authority in the creation and strategic direction of advanced computational methods and architectures for engineering research.
- Deep understanding of performance-aware scientific computing, including the integration of mathematical methods, software architectures, high-performance computing and accessible engineering workflows.
- Proven ability to establish long-term scientific and computational strategy across multiple programmes, platforms and organisations.
- Demonstrable record of introducing state-of-the-art or field-defining computational approaches with major scientific or industrial impact.
- Authority to scrutinise and challenge major computational programmes and advise on the adequacy of their methods, architectures, assurance and long-term direction.

### Engineering & Mathematical Knowledge & Experience

- Internationally recognised expertise in computational experimental design, sensor and measurement modelling, VVUQ, or a closely related discipline, combined with broad authority across the wider field.
- Proven ability to originate new scientific methods, research directions and validation approaches for complex problems with limited precedent.
- Deep understanding of how simulation, experiment, measurement uncertainty and risk-aware decision-making interact across major engineering programmes.
- Demonstrable ability to define what constitutes credible scientific and engineering evidence at programme and organisational level.

### Specialist Knowledge & Experience

- International standing across several of the following areas: simulation-driven or Bayesian experimental design; sensor, imaging or measurement-system modelling; measurement uncertainty and uncertainty propagation; mathematical methods for verification, validation and uncertainty quantification; numerical optimisation, statistical inference or information-theoretic methods; image-based measurement, computational imaging or synthetic data generation; or performance-aware numerical and scientific computing.
- Proven ability to integrate these areas into new scientific capabilities, research fields or strategic programmes with organisation-wide and external impact.

### Research Knowledge & Experience

- A sustained and internationally distinguished record of original research, major publications, invited presentations and demonstrable scientific or industrial impact.
- Proven ability to conceive, establish and lead internationally significant research programmes and long-term scientific visions.
- High national and international standing, evidenced through recognition such as invited leadership roles, advisory appointments, prizes, visiting positions or equivalent distinctions.
- Extensive experience building strategic collaborations with universities, industry, research organisations and international bodies.
- Ability to influence senior organisational leaders, government, funders and external scientific communities on long-term research direction and investment.
- Demonstrable record of developing senior researchers and technical leaders and building enduring organisational capability.

## Additional Duties

- Represent the organisation on major national and international advisory bodies, expert panels and strategic collaborations.
- Undertake national and international travel as required to lead collaborations, represent organisational interests and maintain international scientific leadership.
- Advise and challenge senior leadership on scientific strategy and major programme decisions.
- May hold responsibility for strategic budgets, programmes or organisational appointments, but line management is not a requirement of the role.
- Maintain active external networks with leading universities, research organisations, industry and government bodies.
- Undertake other duties consistent with Senior Fellow status and the strategic scope of the role.

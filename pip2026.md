## Joel - Senior Research Software Engineer

7. Please outline the purpose of the new/proposed role and how it has grown from the current role (max 100 words)

The proposed Grade G Senior Research Software Engineer role provides deep technical leadership for Pyvale’s high-performance compiled software. It will own the architecture, quality and performance of multiple compiled sub-modules integrated into the main Python package, lead performance analysis of critical bottlenecks, set coding and review standards, and provide technical management to graduates and placement students working in aligned areas. The role has grown beyond Level 4 through increased autonomy, cross-module ownership, external representation, technical oversight of others, release responsibility, and leadership of strategically critical software underpinning simulation-driven experimental design for fusion components.

8. Why do you believe that competitive recruitment is not possible for this new role? (max 200 words)

Competitive recruitment is not practicable because the role requires an unusually rare combination of digital image correlation expertise, high-performance C/C++ development, data-oriented design, performance analysis, and detailed knowledge of Pyvale’s architecture. Previous recruitment campaigns have identified candidates with C/C++ or HPC experience, but not this integrated capability; digital image correlation expertise has required substantial internal training.

The role must provide immediate technical ownership of the compiled modules that underpin Pyvale’s open-source DIC capability. This is critical to running large-scale workflows on compute clusters without commercial licence restrictions and to delivering simulation-driven experimental design for fusion components. Appointing externally would require extensive training in both DIC and the existing codebase, creating an estimated minimum 12-month delay. With two guaranteed years remaining on the current fellowship before renewal, that delay would materially threaten delivery, publications and the evidence needed for continued funding.

The required capability already exists within the project and has been demonstrated through delivery of the DIC engine, cross-module review, release management, technical mentoring and external representation. A competitive exercise is therefore highly unlikely to identify a credible alternative able to meet the role’s immediate requirements.

## Wiera - Specialist Research Software Engineer

7. Please outline the purpose of the new/proposed role and how it has grown from the current role (max 100 words)

The proposed Grade F Specialist Research Software Engineer role provides autonomous technical ownership of Pyvale’s path-tracing capability for higher-order finite elements. The role determines the algorithms, data structures and performance strategy needed to deliver agreed project outcomes, while integrating compiled software with the wider Python package. It has grown from Grade E through complete ownership of a specialist codebase, independent selection of technical approaches, responsibility for performance-aware implementation, and peer review across the engineering team. The role will also review and guide work undertaken by graduates and year-in-industry students, while project-wide strategy and objectives remain with the technical lead.

8. Why do you believe that competitive recruitment is not possible for this new role? (max 200 words)

Competitive recruitment is not practicable because the role requires an unusually rare combination of finite-element modelling, path-tracing algorithms, performance-aware compiled software design and Python integration. These capabilities must be applied immediately within Pyvale’s existing architecture to complete and deploy a working beta renderer for quadratic finite elements, which is essential for realistic image simulation in digital image correlation uncertainty studies.

The role was created and recruited before Grade F existed. Its responsibilities have since developed into autonomous ownership of a defined specialist area, matching the new Grade F framework rather than creating a new vacancy. Previous recruitment experience indicates that finding candidates with performance-aware software skills is difficult; combining these with finite-element and path-tracing expertise is substantially more challenging.

An external appointment would require at least 12 months to acquire the necessary domain and codebase knowledge, assuming a suitable candidate were found in the first campaign. A failed campaign could add a further six months. This would materially delay delivery and disrupt continuity in a technically critical workstream. The required specialist capability is already embedded within the project and demonstrably operating at the level the role now requires.

## Khurram - Lead Senior Materials Engineer
7. Please outline the purpose of the new/proposed role and how it has grown from the current role (max 100 words)

The proposed Grade H Lead Senior Materials Engineer role provides strategic leadership for the organisation’s cryo-mechanical testing capability and its contribution to EuroFusion DEMO design criteria. The role defines research questions, technical direction, outputs and funding priorities; controls delivery within agreed budgets; and provides final approval for cryogenic test plans and reports. It coordinates research technical professionals, universities, research technology organisations and international partners, while developing collaborations, co-funded PhDs and external grant proposals. The role has grown beyond Grade G through organisation-wide technical authority, leadership across multiple programmes and disciplines, and external influence on European fusion design rules and wider clean-hydrogen research.


8. Why do you believe that competitive recruitment is not possible for this new role? (max 200 words)

Competitive recruitment is not practicable because the role requires an established combination of organisation-wide authority in cryo-mechanical testing, detailed knowledge of EuroFusion DEMO design-criteria activities, budget and delivery ownership, and trusted relationships across universities, NPL, European partners and the aerospace hydrogen sector. These capabilities are inseparable from the continuity of several active programmes and collaborations.

The role has final technical approval for cryogenic testing plans and reports, coordinates the organisation’s contribution to the DEMO Design Criteria, and directs research technical professionals delivering associated materials-testing campaigns. It also owns the development of a wider research portfolio through co-funded PhDs, inter-laboratory round-robin testing and major external bids. Replacing this capability through open recruitment would require a new appointee to rebuild technical authority, programme knowledge and external trust before exercising the role’s required autonomy.

The necessary capability is already established and demonstrably operating at Grade H scope through organisation-wide coordination, international technical representation, external collaboration and influence on European design rules. A competitive exercise would therefore create significant delivery and relationship risk without a credible prospect of identifying a candidate able to assume immediate responsibility.
